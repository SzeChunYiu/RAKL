## Doctrine

A negative/null/blocked result closes only the failed realization.

For every negative:

1. preserve the epoch byte-for-byte;
2. localize the causal failure stage;
3. saturate the relevant knowledge fibre across disciplines;
4. compile the strongest parent mechanics;
5. change the representation/mechanism, not the post-outcome gate;
6. freeze a materially different successor;
7. execute on fresh/legally reusable evidence;
8. repeat until the failure mechanism is eliminated or an impossibility theorem closes the registered scope.

The target is a **positive successor mechanism**, not a cosmetically positive statistic.
