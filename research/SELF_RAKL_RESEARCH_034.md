# SELF-RAKL Research Round 034 — Senior Researcher Cognitive Architecture and Quantitative Closure

Status: research-only architecture and preregistration update.
Date: 2026-08-09.

## Motivation

The project goal is clarified as a functional emulation of a mature research scientist rather than a monolithic autonomous agent: extensive evidence acquisition should be transformed into reusable explanatory structure, procedural ability, research agenda control, metacognitive error detection, and better scientific actions under bounded context.

## Main diagnosis

Current RAKL already has strong machinery for contextual evidence, authority, gluing/obstruction, negative history, saturation, bounded context, capability shaping, method assimilation, self-evolution and metacognitive gap diagnosis. The largest remaining cognitive layers are:

1. research agenda formation / scientific taste;
2. compact generative understanding measured by held-out reconstruction;
3. mental simulation and counterfactual prediction-set reasoning;
4. consolidation of research episodes into transferable procedural operators;
5. explicit measurement/instrument/calibration cognition;
6. hierarchical multi-timescale programme control;
7. social/collaborative cognition with conceptual-basis diversity;
8. prospective hidden-weakness discovery rather than retrospective repair.

A context-indexed competence tensor is defined:

\[
\mathbf Q(A,M;d,f,t,b,c)=(V,E,D,X,P,G,L,R,C),
\]

covering validity, evidence use/revision, discovery, explanation/mechanism, experimental planning, metacognition/gap detection, learning/self-evolution, robustness/reproducibility and engineering efficiency.

The tensor is Pareto-evaluated under blocking validity constraints; no scalar average may compensate for a blocking scientific regression.

## Paper strategy

The paper should have two independent headline experiments.

### Study A — governed self-evolution

Test whether RAKL can discover hidden weaknesses/missing operators and produce transferable improvements on fresh assurance tasks. Compare fixed RAKL, generic self-reflection/self-editing, development-only evolution, governed evolution, and governed evolution plus external method assimilation.

### Study B — better scientific discovery with the same base LLM

Compare a direct/strong-prompt LLM, retrieval-augmented LLM, strong generic agentic workflow, full RAKL and selective RAKL ablations under matched model/evidence/tools/resources.

Use three evidence tiers: known-answer mechanistic worlds, retrospective time-cutoff real-science discovery, and at least one prospective real scientific case study.

## External evidence routes reviewed

Fresh primary literature reinforces the need to test process rather than workflow completion. Recent work reports that current scientific agents can often complete workflows while failing evidence-sensitive scientific reasoning; capability-oriented benchmarks show continuing difficulty in causal/mechanistic analysis; prospective-hypothesis benchmarks expose model-dependent benefits and regressions from structured skills; experiment-design benchmarks show that search alone is insufficient and that structured stage isolation can help; self-evolution benchmarks emphasize transfer rather than training-task gain.

These results narrow RAKL's claim: the project does not claim to invent scientific agents, structured workflows, self-evolution or research benchmarks. The candidate contribution is an evidence-governed research operating method whose cognitive components, self-improvements and discovery gains are separately measurable.
