# P3 promotion-enforcement status

Frozen expected terminal before CI:

`PROMOTE_CONDITIONALLY_PACKET_ENFORCEMENT_INFRASTRUCTURE`

This means the framework enforcement itself may become incumbent when protected CI is green and historical verdicts are unchanged. It does **not** promote any Paper V/VI scientific mechanic and does not establish that a successor is useful.

P3 succeeds only if:

1. every exact pre-contract candidate keeps its historical verdict;
2. a post-contract positive/conditional candidate without a valid packet is downgraded to `KEEP_PROPOSAL_ONLY`;
3. a new candidate registration without a valid packet produces a preflight/CI problem;
4. a valid packet does not create a positive result — it only allows the ordinary gate result through;
5. applicability, telemetry, evidence and scientific-authority gates remain unchanged;
6. the historical grandfather set is exact and a renamed successor cannot inherit it.

Any failure preserves P1/P2 and reopens the enforcement design.
