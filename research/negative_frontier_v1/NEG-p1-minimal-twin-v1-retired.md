## What happened

The first development instrument used fifteen named failure families and exposed a field called transition_type to every comparator; the value of that field was the family identifier. A RAKL-on-RAKL audit removed the identifier and found that some otherwise identical substantive states carried different gold actions -- the common 'safe' state was interpreted as promotion in most families but as hold or scope restriction in others, and an otherwise identical superseded state was assigned different revocation/supersession actions in two families. The first panel therefore did not define one coherent function a* over the scientific state unless family identity was treated as answer-semantic input. Its numerical separation result is retained as development history and is NOT used as flagship evidence.
