## What happened

A label-blind power study registered a primary paired Brier-reduction MDE of 0.05 and simulated paired bootstrap/sign-flip resolution under plausible item-level noise. The frozen 16-item v2.1 packet is underpowered for that MDE: adequate resolution requires roughly n~48 items across the registered noise envelope. Consequences carried in the manuscript: indistinguishable outcomes are inconclusive rather than negative evidence; confirmatory refutation claims are blocked when intervals include zero; the power limitation is not repaired by the demoted AI-operator path; the item set cannot be enlarged post-label and represented as the same confirmatory epoch.
