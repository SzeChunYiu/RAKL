# TCSQ cross-paper ownership

The handoff contains useful extensions for the full RAKL publication series, but the mechanism must have one scientific owner until evidence justifies broader inheritance.

## Paper I — Epistemic Mechanics

**Implement now:** authority/non-destructive-view boundary only.

Owned statement:

> Computational erasure from a task-conditioned derived view is not epistemic absence, retraction, falsity or authority loss in the canonical source. A derived quotient can inherit but cannot mint scientific authority; quotient-side success still requires ordinary original-problem verification/promotion.

Do not add solver-efficiency or abstraction-quality claims to Paper I.

## Paper II — Structural Mechanics

**Primary owner.**

Owns:
- TCSQ proposal and validation objects;
- preserved/erased/conditional coordinate partition;
- protected coordinates, preserved invariants and forbidden losses;
- sufficiency obligations, falsifiers and fail-closed unknowns;
- reconstruction and original-problem verification contract;
- relationship `raw problem -> validated quotient -> structural object -> directional witness`;
- SQ0-SQ5 experiments.

## Paper III — Method-Evolution Mechanics

**Defer manuscript integration until a fresh policy experiment exists.**

Candidate later question:

> Does persistent experience help RAKL choose better quotient policies on fresh tasks than a static quotient proposer?

Minimum comparison:
`NO_QUOTIENT / STATIC_TCSQ / EXPERIENCE_CONDITIONED_TCSQ`, with a fresh evaluation epoch and total cost. Do not infer method evolution from storing quotient records alone.

## Paper IV — Structural Learning Mechanics (conditional slot)

**No change now.**

Only consider a latent/internalized TCSQ claim if the existing Paper-IV training gate independently opens and if a weight-updating learner exhibits a distinct learner-conditioned quotient/saturation phenomenon beyond the Paper-II inference-time mechanism.

## Paper V — Verified Discovery in Mathematics

**Application after the core mechanism is stable.**

A useful later high-control test is exact theorem/statement reduction where nuisance notation or representation is quotientable, followed by reconstruction and proof checking of the original statement. The theorem authority remains owned by the existing specification/truth/verifier coordinates; quotient validity cannot substitute for proof.

## Paper VI — RAKL Scientific Research Engine

**Capstone inheritance only.**

Paper VI may later show TCSQ as one optional stage in the end-to-end runtime:
`canonical state -> target-conditioned view -> fibre/router -> result -> reconstruction -> scientific verifier`.
Do not give Paper VI an independent TCSQ novelty claim.

## Anti-salami rule

TCSQ is not a reason to create another manuscript. A standalone paper becomes justifiable only if later experiments reveal a distinct scientific phenomenon not adequately owned by Paper II (for example, a general law of learned quotient policy or a verified-mathematics result with its own central estimand). Until then, all TCSQ mechanics belong to Paper II and the other papers receive only their necessary boundary/application references.
