# p2-template-inversion — the successor is refused before it is built

**This session cannot build an admissible successor.** Not for want of effort: the lever requires
something a single author cannot supply.

## The lever

> successor prose instrument: renderer author != extractor author; held-out realization per
> ambiguity class; ceiling must not rise

The predecessor failed G3 because all 55 coordinate errors fell into exactly one of seven registered
ambiguity classes — the surface was invertible by construction, because the renderer and the
extractor shared an author.

## The gate refused it, prospectively

The design this session could actually produce was put through the construct-independence gate
merged in #731 **before** any instrument was built:

```text
instrument  p2-prose-successor-v2-single-author
verdict     INADMISSIBLE
violated    AUTHOR_SEPARATION
chain       REVISE_MEASUREMENT
```

Three of the four obligations hold. Channel separation holds — gold coordinates never appear in the
rendered surface. Gold independence holds — gold is a function of the registered structural task.
The permutation null holds — scrambling collapses exact accuracy, as in the predecessor.

**Author separation cannot hold.** One agent writing both the renderer and the extractor is exactly
the condition that produced the original defect, and no amount of care inside a single author
substitutes for a second one.

This is the gate working the way it was specified to: refusing an instrument *before* the epoch is
spent, rather than after a battery discovers the surface was invertible. The predecessor was
diagnosed retrospectively; this successor is refused prospectively.

## Consequence: reclassification

```text
p2-template-inversion   REVIVABLE_LOCAL  ->  REVIVABLE_EXTERNAL
```

Author separation is a **resource**, in the same sense as an A100 or a third-party annotator. It is
not a budget question and no quantity of local effort supplies it. Classing this record as locally
revivable was an error the frontier could not have caught, because the obligation it violates was
only written down today.

## What would change it

1. A **second author** — a human, or a separate agent with no access to the renderer — writing the
   extractor against the rendered surface alone.
2. Or a **third-party corpus** whose surfaces this programme did not render, which converts the
   obligation from author separation into ordinary provenance.

## Support, recorded for whoever builds it

```text

conditioning variable   ambiguity_class  (all 55 errors fell in one of seven)
reachable ceiling       0.9722222222222222
ceiling basis           G2 full_exact on the executed predecessor run
```

The ceiling is the constraint a successor **must not exceed**, not a target to reach: a successor
scoring higher than the invertible predecessor would be evidence of a new inversion, not of better
extraction.

## Not claimed

A successor instrument is not shown impossible — only that this session cannot build an admissible
one. The predecessor terminal `INSTRUMENT_NOT_PROBATIVE__TEMPLATE_INVERSION` is unchanged.
