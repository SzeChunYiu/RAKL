## Atomic question

Can RAKL distinguish four events that were previously too easy to blur together?

1. the incumbent method basis failed;
2. the blocking epistemic cut was localized;
3. a missing operator family was identified;
4. that operator transferred to a fresh realization rather than merely fitting one development world.

The central rule is:

```text
gap detection != operator identification != fresh transfer != method authority
```

## Six-role panel

The role-separated panel was used for breadth, not counted as independent assurance.

### Cognitive science / analogy

Primary concern: an apparent operator discovery can be a surface-specific trick. A transferable discovery therefore requires a fresh surface-shifted or domain-shifted realization. This role and the adversarial reviewer jointly required explicit transfer failure to remain only partial identification.

### Knowledge representation / ontology

Primary concern: a generic `METHOD_BASIS_GAP_CANDIDATE` does not identify what is missing. The benchmark now treats `gap_detected`, `epistemic_cut_id`, and `candidate_operator_family` as different coordinates. This role and applied mathematics jointly required non-identifiability to remain explicit when several operator families fit the same evidence.

### Scientific information retrieval

Primary concern: retrieval can suggest vocabulary or candidate methods but can also leak the answer. Hidden operator-family labels are evaluator-only. Leakage through either the prompt or retrieved material invalidates the trial. This role and the adversarial reviewer jointly rejected retrieval success as a resolution witness.

### Applied mathematics / dynamical systems

Primary concern: the sealed world must contain a discriminating cut. If the incumbent and candidate operator families make no distinguishable frozen prediction/action on the available probes, operator identity is not established. A candidate that fits one world but leaves alternative operator families compatible remains `PARTIALLY_IDENTIFIED`.

### Computational creativity / search

Primary concern: candidate diversity is useful exploration but not evidence. The benchmark scores resolution and fresh transfer, not novelty of wording or number of proposed methods. This role and cognitive science jointly rejected novelty-only credit.

### Adversarial scientific method

Primary concern: benchmark chronology and authority. Evaluator/answer-key freeze, candidate-definition freeze, matched resources, negative-history preservation, no hidden-label leakage, and authority non-escalation are blocking coordinates. Same-orchestration role separation receives no independent-review credit.
