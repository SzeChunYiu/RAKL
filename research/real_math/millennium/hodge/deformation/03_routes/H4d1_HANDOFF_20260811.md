## New child residual: H4d1a

The first lifting gap is now sharper than “generalize semiregularity.”

`H4d1a` — **branch-restricted semiregularity-envelope problem**:

For a fixed chosen witness and Hodge-locus branch `T`, construct a geometrically computable obstruction envelope `B_{T,m}` at every required infinitesimal order such that:

1. every obstruction generated by attempts to lift the witness along `T` lies in `B_{T,m}`;
2. the Hodge/semiregularity detector is injective on `B_{T,m}`;
3. `B_{T,m}` is defined independently of the desired obstruction vanishing;
4. the condition is demonstrably weaker than full semiregularity on a nontrivial family, or the attempted weakening is recorded as failed;
5. the construction is stable enough across Artin orders to support formal lifting.

The hard part is not the elementary implication “detector zero + injectivity => obstruction zero”; it is finding a non-tautological, checkable, branch-generated envelope and proving the containment/injectivity statements.

Even a successful H4d1a result would still leave algebraization, global continuation, and root initial algebraicity open.
