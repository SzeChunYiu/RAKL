## Official root statement

For an elliptic curve `E/Q`, the official Clay problem description by Andrew Wiles states that the Taylor expansion of `L(E,s)` at `s=1` has order equal to `rank E(Q)`. The refined form relates the first nonzero coefficient to the real period, regulator, Tamagawa/local factors, torsion, and the order of the Tate–Shafarevich group.

Primary source: Andrew Wiles, *The Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Conjecture*, Clay Mathematics Institute, official Millennium problem description.

## Exact lane boundary

This lane studies Selmer groups, descent, cyclotomic/anticyclotomic Iwasawa theory, p-adic L-functions, Euler/Kolyvagin systems, Selmer complexes, local conditions, regulators/heights, Sha, congruences, and explicit reciprocity.

It must distinguish:

- algebraic rank from complex analytic rank;
- `p^∞`-Selmer corank from Mordell–Weil rank when Sha-divisibility is not controlled;
- one-prime `p`-primary information from the global all-primes arithmetic factor;
- p-adic leading terms from the complex leading Taylor coefficient;
- low-rank converses from arbitrary-rank statements;
- conditional main-conjecture consequences from unconditional theorems.

## First atom `BSD-S001`

Find the smallest extra mathematical object or exact comparison identity needed to convert arbitrary-rank p-primary Selmer/Iwasawa structure into:

1. `ord_{s=1} L(E,s) = rank E(Q)` for arbitrary rank; and
2. the full refined complex leading-term formula.

No candidate bridge is present in the initial packet. The first strict packet only freezes the context, transfer matrix, expert objections, experience review, and next discriminator.

## Success and failure conditions for this atom

A successful child result must either:

- prove a genuinely new, correctly scoped bridge from registered p-primary arithmetic data to complex analytic rank or the global leading term; or
- prove a route-pruning theorem showing a specified class of proposed bridges cannot be sufficient, with exact scope.
