# YM-E1a primary-source packet — 2026-08-11

**Scope:** source binding for the first constructive-continuum atom. This is not a proof, novelty certificate or endorsement of any claimed Millennium solution.

## S0 — exact root statement

Arthur Jaffe and Edward Witten, *Quantum Yang–Mills Theory*, official Clay Mathematics Institute problem description.

- https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf
- Section 4 requires, for any compact simple gauge group `G`, a non-trivial quantum Yang–Mills theory on `R^4` with a mass gap `Δ>0`, with existence satisfying axiomatic properties at least as strong as the cited Wightman / Osterwalder–Schrader schemes.
- The same section defines the mass gap spectrally: no Hamiltonian spectrum in `(0,Δ)` above the vacuum for some `Δ>0`.
- Section 6.5 states that Wilson lattice regularization is reflection positive and that one must establish limits of gauge-invariant observable expectations as lattice spacing tends to zero and volume tends to infinity.
- The discussion of Balaban says the estimates are an important step toward a compact continuum limit but need extension to expectations of gauge-invariant field functions.
- Clay explicitly excludes weak-existence/compactness alone as a solution unless further techniques establish the axioms and properties such as a mass gap.

## S1 — fixed-cutoff reflection positivity

K. Osterwalder and E. Seiler, *Gauge theories on a lattice*, Annals of Physics **110** (1978), 440–471.

**Retained use:** the Wilson lattice formulation is a positivity-preserving regulator and therefore a natural source context for OS reconstruction.

**Non-use:** fixed-cutoff reflection positivity is not a continuum-limit theorem and does not itself identify a common continuum observable algebra.

## S2 — 4D pure-gauge multiscale control

T. Balaban, *Renormalization group approach to lattice gauge field theories. I. Generation of effective actions in a small field approximation and a coupling constant renormalization in four dimensions*, Communications in Mathematical Physics **109** (1987), 249–301.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01215223

T. Balaban, *Large field renormalization. II. Localization, exponentiation, and bounds for the R operation*, Communications in Mathematical Physics **122** (1989), 355–392.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01238433

**Retained use:** four-dimensional pure-gauge RG, asymptotically-free coupling flow, localized effective actions and small/large-field ultraviolet estimates are the closest same-problem multiscale source context.

**Boundary:** ultraviolet stability of effective actions/normalization does not by itself establish convergence, identification, locality or non-triviality of a separating family of renormalized gauge-invariant observables.

## S3 — higher-dimensional non-Abelian scaling-limit analogue

Sourav Chatterjee, *A scaling limit of SU(2) lattice Yang–Mills–Higgs theory*, arXiv:2401.10507.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10507

**Retained use:** provides a rigorous example in dimension `d>=2` where non-Abelian lattice gauge variables are quantitatively identified with a continuum field under an explicit scaling regime.

**Critical disanalogy:** the source includes a fundamental Higgs field; unitary gauge and the Higgs scale are load-bearing; the proved limit in the stated regime is massive Gaussian; the paper explicitly leaves construction of a non-Gaussian scaling limit open. It therefore cannot be used as evidence for pure 4D Yang–Mills existence or mass gap.

## S4 — gauge-covariant singular renormalization analogue

A. Chandra, I. Chevyrev, M. Hairer, H. Shen, *Stochastic quantisation of Yang–Mills–Higgs in 3D*, Inventiones Mathematicae **237** (2024), 541–696.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00222-024-01264-2
- Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.03487

**Retained use:** constructs a distributional state space, extends gauge equivalence, and obtains a renormalized gauge-covariant-in-law Markov process on gauge orbits in 3D, showing that gauge symmetry can remain explicit at singular regularity.

**Critical disanalogy:** dimension three, Yang–Mills–Higgs rather than pure 4D YM, and the construction is local in time up to possible finite-time blow-up; it does not itself give the required 4D invariant Euclidean measure, OS reconstruction, or mass gap.

## S5 — lower-dimensional constructive-QFT calibration

Jaffe–Witten section 6 summarizes the classical constructive-QFT examples (including Glimm–Jaffe and related work) where non-Gaussian Euclidean measures, Schwinger functions, Wightman axioms and particle/mass properties can be constructed in two and three dimensions.

**Retained use:** method-level calibration that continuum QFT existence is an observable/correlation-function problem, not merely control of partition functions.

**Critical disanalogy:** these scalar theories are lower-dimensional and typically superrenormalizable; they lack non-Abelian gauge redundancy and the critical four-dimensional running-coupling structure.

## Current frontier caution

A web/arXiv search on 2026-08-11 also returns recent manuscripts that claim complete 4D Yang–Mills constructions and mass gaps. They are **not** used as positive authority in this packet. Clay still lists Yang–Mills & the Mass Gap as unsolved, and any claimed solution must undergo exact theorem-level dependency, verifier, novelty and genuinely independent review before it can affect root status.

## Source-to-claim boundary

This packet supports only the following pre-candidate claims:

1. the root contract and spectral meaning of the gap;
2. fixed-cutoff Wilson reflection positivity;
3. existence of strong 4D pure-gauge RG/UV-stability machinery that does not itself close the full observable continuum problem;
4. existence of nearby rigorous gauge-theory scaling/renormalization results whose broken assumptions are explicit;
5. selection of observable-level cutoff removal as a legitimate high-information constructive-continuum atom.

It supports **no** claim that 4D pure Yang–Mills has been constructed and **no** claim of a mass gap.
