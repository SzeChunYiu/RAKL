# Riemann Hypothesis — canonical root statement

## Object

The Riemann zeta function is defined for `Re(s) > 1` by

`zeta(s) = sum_{n>=1} n^{-s}`

and by its meromorphic continuation elsewhere, with its standard functional equation. Its zeros at the negative even integers are the trivial zeros.

## Root statement

**Riemann Hypothesis (RH).** Every nontrivial zero `rho` of `zeta(s)` satisfies

`Re(rho) = 1/2`.

Equivalently, with Riemann's completed `xi`-function in the standard normalization, the corresponding zeros in the `t` variable are all real.

This is the exact root problem tracked by this workspace. Generalized RH for other L-functions, a density theorem, a positive critical-line proportion, a finite-height verification, a zero-free region, a Li/Nyman/Weil equivalent criterion without its universal condition proved, or a theorem conditional on RH is not the root solution.
