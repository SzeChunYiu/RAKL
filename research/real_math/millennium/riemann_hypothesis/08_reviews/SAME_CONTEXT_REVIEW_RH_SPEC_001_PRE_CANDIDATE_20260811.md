The six roles below review the same frozen context. They are deliberately separated by expertise and adversarial responsibility, but they share the same research context and therefore do **not** satisfy an independent-review requirement.

## 1. Analytic-number-theory lead

**Background:** zeta/L-functions, explicit formulas, prime-number theory, zero criteria.

**Finding:** the lane should bind itself to the exact completed-zeta statement and Weil's sign criterion rather than to GUE statistics, `N(T)`, or finite zero computations.

**Strongest objection:** an alleged positive quadratic form can be circular because Weil's sign condition is already equivalent to RH. Merely renaming that sign condition as an inner product creates no new structure.

**Delegated obligation:** for every future factorization, identify the exact admissible test-function class, moment constraints, prime-power terms, archimedean terms, and the independently derived transform whose positivity is claimed.

**Vote:** `REVISE_BEFORE_CANDIDATE`.

## 2. Spectral/operator lead

**Background:** unbounded operators, self-adjoint extensions, spectral theorem, trace ideals and distributions.

**Finding:** “find a Hermitian operator with the zeros” is too coarse. The state space and operator domain are part of the theorem, not technical cleanup.

**Strongest objection:** a symmetric formal differential expression can have many self-adjoint extensions, continuous spectrum, or no legitimate trace; boundary conditions can encode the target spectrum.

**Delegated obligation:** future routes must separately prove domain density, closedness/closability, self-adjointness or positivity at the exact needed authority, spectral type, trace/distribution legitimacy, and completeness/multiplicity of the zero correspondence.

**Vote:** `REVISE_BEFORE_CANDIDATE`.

## 3. Arithmetic-geometry / trace-formula lead

**Background:** Frobenius/cohomology analogues, Selberg-style trace formulas, arithmetic dynamics.

**Finding:** the finite-field and Selberg cases are valuable because the operator/geometric object exists independently of the desired zeros and the trace relation is exact.

**Strongest objection:** importing the vocabulary “Frobenius”, “periodic orbit”, or “prime geodesic” without reproducing the exact Riemann prime-power and archimedean weights only renames the obstruction.

**Delegated obligation:** use the solved analogues to build an obligation matrix: independently defined state space, exact trace identity, arithmetic weight match, positivity/duality mechanism, and spectrum completeness.

**Vote:** `REVISE_BEFORE_CANDIDATE`.

## 4. Adversarial functional-analysis lead

**Background:** pathological operators, noncompact dynamics, generalized spectra, regularized determinants/traces.

**Finding:** the cheapest falsifiers should precede symbolic operator construction.

**Strongest objection:** the spectral literature permits visually compelling formulas that fail at domain, convergence, continuous-spectrum, or regularization steps. A kernel or boundary condition reconstructed from known zeros is especially dangerous.

**Delegated falsifiers:** reject a candidate immediately if (a) its domain contains zero-location data, (b) the trace drops an unproved correction/continuous-spectrum term, (c) self-adjointness is shown only formally, or (d) it matches only mean density/statistics.

**Vote:** `BLOCK_CANDIDATE_UNTIL_OBLIGATION_MATRIX`.

## 5. Formal-methods lead

**Background:** statement binding, proof DAGs, formal verification interfaces.

**Finding:** `RH-SPEC-001` is still a bridge-specification atom, not an operator-invention atom.

**Strongest objection:** without a common obligation matrix, different route families can silently change what “spectral realization” means and make later verification incomparable.

**Delegated obligation:** freeze a machine-readable or testable matrix with at least: state space/domain; self-adjointness/positivity; trace legitimacy; exact prime/archimedean matching; zero multiplicity/completeness; non-circularity.

**Vote:** `REVISE_BEFORE_CANDIDATE`.

## 6. Novelty / research-value lead

**Finding:** Hilbert–Polya, Weil positivity, trace-formula, Connes, and Berry–Keating ideas are longstanding. Novelty cannot be inferred from recombining their vocabulary.

**Strongest objection:** a future lemma may be a useful route checkpoint yet already known as an equivalent RH criterion or standard operator fact.

**Delegated obligation:** postpone novelty promotion until an exact proved statement exists, then search notation-normalized equivalents and stronger parents.

**Vote:** `REVISE_BEFORE_CANDIDATE`.
