## 3. Vorticity / strain-geometry lead

**Background:** vorticity equation, strain eigenframes, enstrophy growth, geometric depletion.

**Delegated derivation:** construct exact calibration seeds.

Take
`S0 = diag(-1/2,-1/2,1)` and
`Ω(b) = [[0,-b,0],[b,0,0],[0,0,0]]`.
For `A(b)=S0+Ω(b)`, `tr A=0`, `ω=2b e3`, `S0 ω=ω`, and `ω·S0ω=4b^2>0`.

For the finite-energy seed, take `b=1`, `u0(x)=A(1)x`, vector potential
`a0(x)=-(1/3) x×u0(x)`, and `φ=curl(χ a0)` with a standard smooth cutoff `χ=1` on `B1`, compactly supported in `B2`. Then `φ=u0` on `B1`, so the vorticity direction is exactly constant there and aligned with the positive strain eigenvector.

For the exact-dynamics calibration, let `b(t)=b0 e^t`, `u(x,t)=A(b(t))x`. Since `A'+A^2=diag(1/4-b^2,1/4-b^2,1)` is symmetric, the quadratic pressure
`p=-1/2 x·(A'+A^2)x`
makes `(u,p)` an exact smooth Navier–Stokes solution with `Δu=0`. It has perfect positive-eigenframe alignment and exponential vorticity growth, but infinite kinetic energy on `R^3`.

**Strongest objection:** neither calibration belongs to the full Clay class for the same reason: A2 has finite energy but is not a trajectory; A3 is a trajectory but lacks finite energy/decay.

**Vote:** `ACCEPT`.

## 4. Adversarial falsification lead

**Finding:** the pair A2/A3 partitions two hidden assumptions. A2 defeats “energy + incompressibility + local alignment implies depletion.” A3 defeats “exact PDE + local alignment alone implies depletion/regularity in the Clay sense.” Their intersection points to the missing joint coordinate: exact evolution **and** finite-energy/global localization.

**Strongest objection:** A3 is not a counterexample to any finite-energy theorem and A2 is not a counterexample to an equation-specific theorem.

**Vote:** `ACCEPT`; promote only the scope diagnosis, not a theorem.
