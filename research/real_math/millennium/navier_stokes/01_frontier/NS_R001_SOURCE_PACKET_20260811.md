# NS-R001 primary-source frontier packet — 2026-08-11

This packet binds the first regularity atom to primary/authoritative sources. It is not a literature-completeness or novelty certificate.

## Official root

Charles L. Fefferman, official Clay Mathematics Institute problem description, *Existence and Smoothness of the Navier–Stokes Equation*. The document states the `R^3` and periodic existence/smoothness alternatives and the two breakdown alternatives.

## Partial-regularity / critical-control anchors

- L. Caffarelli, R. Kohn, L. Nirenberg, *Partial regularity of suitable weak solutions of the Navier-Stokes equations*, Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 35 (1982), 771–831, DOI `10.1002/cpa.3160350604`. Use: scale-local epsilon/partial regularity; does not turn global energy into all-scale smallness.
- L. Escauriaza, G. A. Seregin, V. Sverak, *L_{3,infinity}-solutions of the Navier–Stokes equations and backward uniqueness*, Russian Math. Surveys 58 (2003), 211–250, DOI `10.1070/RM2003v058n02ABEH000609`. Use: endpoint critical `L_t^∞L_x^3` boundedness excludes blowup; hypothesis generation remains open.
- H. Koch, D. Tataru, *Well-posedness for the Navier-Stokes equations*, Advances in Mathematics 157 (2001), 22–35, DOI `10.1006/aima.2000.1937`. Use: small-data global well-posedness in critical `BMO^{-1}`; does not cover arbitrary large smooth data.
- C. E. Kenig, G. S. Koch, *An alternative approach to regularity for the Navier-Stokes equations in critical spaces*, arXiv:0908.3349. Use: concentration-compactness/rigidity under bounded critical `dot H^{1/2}` control.
- T. Barker, C. Prange, *Quantitative regularity for the Navier-Stokes equations via spatial concentration*, arXiv:2003.06717. Use: quantitative concentration information under Type-I/critical bounds.

## Negative structural calibration

T. Tao, *Finite time blowup for an averaged three-dimensional Navier-Stokes equation*, JAMS 29 (2016), arXiv:1402.0290. The averaged model retains the energy cancellation identity but admits finite-time blowup. Therefore energy identity plus generic harmonic-analysis structure is not, by itself, a sufficient proof basis for true 3D Navier–Stokes regularity.

## Current frontier interpretation

The selected atom is deliberately narrower than “prove regularity.” Existing theory supplies powerful implications **from** critical control to regularity. The unresolved bridge is to derive such control from arbitrary smooth finite-energy data without assuming it. The next strict step should discriminate exact-nonlinearity mechanisms rather than add another conditional criterion.
