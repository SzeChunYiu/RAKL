# P versus NP — RAKL flagship 001

**Root target:** prove either `P = NP` or `P != NP` under standard deterministic/nondeterministic Turing-machine definitions, with the current working hypothesis focusing on `P != NP`.

**Current authority:** OPEN_PROBLEM / NO_SOLUTION_CERTIFICATE.

## Active research cell

These are analytical roles, not claims of independent human reviewers.

1. **Complexity theorist lens**. Owns reductions, circuit/proof complexity, class containments, and meaningful bridge theorems.
2. **Barrier specialist lens**. Attempts to classify every proposed route against relativization, natural-proofs, algebrization, and known restricted-model gaps.
3. **Computational mathematician lens**. Builds exact small-instance oracles and counterexample-first tests.
4. **Formal-methods lens**. Owns exact statement binding, proof DAGs, dependency audits, and machine-checkable lemmas.
5. **Adversarial novelty lens**. Searches for neighboring known results, direction errors, hidden uniformity changes, and false claims of progress.

## Research strategy

We do not repeatedly ask a language model to write a complete proof. We maintain a persistent DAG and attack the smallest uncertified obstruction.

Current route tournament:

- `R001` monotone CLIQUE lower bounds -> isolate the negation gap;
- `R002` meta-complexity / MCSP -> seek hardness-magnification bridge obligations that admit exact finite tests;
- `R003` circuit-state dual potentials -> synthesize lower-bound invariants from exact circuit state graphs, then test whether any compressed invariant can scale without immediately falling into a known barrier.

`R003` is the current discovery engine. It is not itself a claimed path to P != NP.
