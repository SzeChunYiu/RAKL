# R004 route delta after C014

**Date:** 2026-08-11  
**Authority:** route-control update only

## Result integrated

C014 adds maximum degree as a first-line upper-bound adversary for full graph cover complexity.

For any non-trivial bipartite graph `G subseteq [N] x [N]` with maximum degree `Delta` and `k=ceil(log_2 N)`, the explicit matching-decomposition construction gives

`rho(G,G_{N,N}) <= D_intersection(G | G_{N,N}) <= Delta*(2k+1)`.

The proof is constructive at the set-operation level:

1. regularize the bipartite graph to a `Delta`-regular multigraph and iteratively extract perfect matchings using Hall, giving at most `Delta` matchings after dummy edges are removed;
2. extend each matching to a perfect matching of the ambient complete bipartite graph;
3. encode perfect-matching pairs by distinct `k`-bit strings and construct equality one bit at a time using one intersection per bit;
4. intersect the bit-equality sets, then mask down to a partial matching when necessary;
5. union the constructed matchings, which adds no counted intersections in `D_intersection`.

## Search consequence

All bounded-degree graph families are retired as super-logarithmic R004 targets, regardless of whether their adjacency predicate looks nonlinear, pseudorandom, algebraic, or globally correlated.

More generally, every candidate must record its maximum degree before lower-bound search. The simple ceiling

`Delta*(2 ceil(log_2 N)+1)`

is now part of the upper-bound screen. It may be loose for growing degree, but any candidate already reduced to `O(log N)` by this bound is dead for the current positive goal.

## O9d6 disposition

O9d6 asked for nonlinear globally correlated graph families that survive the C013 quotient/twin attack. C014 shows that surviving quotient compression is not enough: sparse/bounded-degree families are also automatically cheap.

O9d6 is therefore refined by C014.

## New active atom O9d7

Search only among graph families that simultaneously have:

- no low-cover twin/identical-neighbourhood quotient;
- growing maximum degree;
- no immediate `O(log N)` source-level intersection or cyclic construction;
- no obvious pair-multiplexing or generator-separation cover;
- tractable small instances for exact full-cover falsification when possible.

The discriminator order becomes:

1. quotient/twin compression;
2. maximum-degree / matching-decomposition ceiling;
3. exact tiny full-cover search when the complement guard permits it;
4. explicit `D_intersection` and cyclic-construction upper bounds;
5. pair multiplexing / generator separation;
6. only then asymptotic lower-bound search.

Candidate generators may include growing-degree algebraic incidence relations, nonlinear code correlations, and pseudorandom explicit relations. Their naming or algebraic complexity provides no authority; only survival of the upper-bound tournament warrants lower-bound effort.

## Root boundary

C014 is route pruning only. It does not prove a super-logarithmic graph-cover lower bound, a Boolean circuit lower bound, or `P != NP`. Root status remains `OPEN_NO_SOLUTION_CERTIFICATE`.
