# R004 route delta after C009

**Date:** 2026-08-10  
**Authority:** route-control update only

## Source-bound context

Cavalar–Oliveira, *Boolean Circuit Complexity and Two-Dimensional Cover Problems*, ECCC TR25-033 / ACM TOCT 17(2), 2025, define full cover complexity through all relevant semi-filters. Their Section 4.2 introduces the smaller canonical family and proves `rho_can(G_NEQ)=log_2 N` for `N=2^n`. Their Theorem 37 proves `Theta(N)` full cover complexity for random bipartite graphs.

## Canonical lane closed as a super-logarithmic target

C009 proves at proof-draft authority that for every square graph `G subseteq [N] x [N]`, with complement `U=G^c` and maximum matching number `nu(U)`,

`rho_can(G) <= ceil(log_2 nu(U)) <= ceil(log_2 N)`.

The proof partitions the active endpoints of `U` into star-biclique classes indexed by a maximum matching, then binary-codes those classes. Cross-class complement edges are placed in both members of each pair, while internal class edges provide the exclusive witnesses required by the exact C005 canonical criterion.

Therefore no Hall-deficient, expander, random-like, algebraic, Cayley, or other square graph family can produce a super-logarithmic **canonical** lower bound. The canonical oracle remains useful only for regression, finite falsification, and theorem calibration.

## Why R004 remains active

C009 does not upper-bound full cover complexity. The source full problem quantifies over every relevant semi-filter, not merely the canonical row/column-generated one. Merged C008 already gives an explicit `3 x 3` graph with

`rho_can(G)=1 < 2=rho(G,G_{3,3})`.

Thus the canonical lane was genuinely losing information.

## Active successor

The highest-value R004 obligation remains O9d1:

> construct or refute a composition theorem for C008-like full-cover gadgets that forces asymptotic growth without collapsing under cross-gadget pair reuse.

Counterexample-first work should test whether a single global pair can simultaneously cover lifted hard semi-filters from many gadget coordinates. A composition theorem is not credible until this pair-reuse obstruction is explicitly controlled.
