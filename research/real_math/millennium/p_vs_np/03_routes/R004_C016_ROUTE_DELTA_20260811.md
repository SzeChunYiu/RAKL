## New upper-bound adversary

For a fixed local map `h:A x A -> Sigma` and a fixed DFA `M=(Q,q0,delta,F)`, the induced length-`t` graph family satisfies

`rho(G_t) <= t (C_h + |Q||Sigma|)`,

where `C_h` is the total fixed local intersection cost of the symbol partition.

Thus fixed-state coordinate aggregation gives `rho=O(log N)` for `N=|A|^t`.

This strictly extends the route lesson of C012. Parity was one special two-state recurrence; C016 applies to every fixed deterministic finite-state aggregation rule with fixed local symbol cost.

## Counterexample to the idea that growing arboricity is enough

The explicit graph

`G_t = {(x,y): HammingDistance(x,y) == 0 mod 3}`

has three-state aggregation and therefore `rho(G_t) <= 8 log2 N` by the unoptimized C016 construction.

At the same time every row has degree

`D_t = sum_{j=0 mod 3} binom(t,j) = (2^t + 2 cos(pi t/3))/3`,

so total edges are `N D_t`. Since one forest on the `2N` vertices has at most `2N-1` edges, its edge arboricity is at least

`ceil(N D_t/(2N-1)) = Omega(N)`.

Therefore C015's growing-arboricity requirement is necessary only as a screen; it is far from sufficient.
