## C022 update

A classical restricted-model lower bound is now bound to the exact same QR relation rather than to a loosely named Paley sibling.

Using the exact `+/-1` incidence matrix `A_p` of `QR_p`, C022 proves

`||A_p|| = sqrt(p+1)` when `p=3 mod 4`,

and

`||A_p|| = sqrt(p)+1` when `p=1 mod 4`.

Lokam 2003, Theorem 4.7 therefore yields

`L3_B(QR_p) = Omega((log p)^3/(log log p)^5)`

for depth-3 bipartite formulas.

Together with C021, this creates an explicit asymptotic gap between a strong restricted-depth lower bound and a much smaller unrestricted intersection upper bound for the same graph.

## Route decision

The operator-norm/sign-rank depth-3 method is retained as a **diagnostic parent**, not an active direct lower-bound route for `rho`.

Reason: any direct scale-preserving transfer to unrestricted cover complexity would contradict the already recorded C021 upper envelope. The missing object is not another proof that QR has pseudorandom spectrum; that property is already strong enough to make the restricted model hard.

The missing object is a lower-bound quantity stable under the extra power of unrestricted reuse/cyclic fusion.
