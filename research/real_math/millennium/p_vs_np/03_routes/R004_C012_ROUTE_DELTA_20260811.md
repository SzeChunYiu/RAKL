## Result integrated

C012 tests the first O9d4 family whose complement is globally correlated rather than a Cartesian product. For a fixed local predicate `q` aggregated by parity over `t` coordinates, the graph-membership predicate and its complement can be maintained by a two-intersection XOR/XNOR recurrence.

If

`a = D_intersection(q^{-1}(1) | B)`

and

`b = D_intersection(q^{-1}(0) | B)`,

then the source transference theorem gives

`rho(G_t) <= t(a+b) + 2(t-1)`.

For a fixed local alphabet and predicate this is `O(t)=O(log N)`. In particular the mod-2 inner-product graph has the explicit ceiling

`rho <= 3t-2`.

Thus simple parity correlation escapes C011's factorization hypothesis but still fails the R004 super-logarithmic target.

## Upper-bound adversary strengthened

The earlier `sigma(G)` generator-separation screen remains useful but is incomplete for correlated complements. A compact non-disjoint intersection construction may certify a small full cover even when no low-cardinality disjoint separator family is obvious.

Every future R004 candidate must therefore face two independent upper-bound attacks before lower-bound search:

1. a direct generator-separation / pair-multiplexing construction;
2. the best explicit intersection or cyclic-construction circuit for its graph-membership predicate, converted to a cover bound through the primary-source transference theorem.

A candidate with `D_intersection = O(log N)` is immediately retired as a super-log cover target.

## New active atom O9d5

Move from fixed finite-state aggregators to **growing global constraints**. The first candidate class is a code/syndrome-constrained relation in which the check rank or global constraint complexity grows with the coordinate count.

For each proposed family freeze:

- exact vertex encoding and side size `N`;
- graph and complement predicates;
- check matrix / code / syndrome parameters;
- the strongest explicit `D_intersection` upper bound for membership and its construction;
- finite counterexamples to any claimed pair lower bound;
- the exact threshold at which the source Proposition 38 would turn a surviving cover lower bound into a circuit lower bound.

Do not spend asymptotic lower-bound budget until the membership predicate itself survives the `O(log N)` intersection-complexity screen.
