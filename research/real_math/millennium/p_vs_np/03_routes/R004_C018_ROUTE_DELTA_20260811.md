# R004 route delta — C018 GF(2)-rank pullback ceiling

**Date:** 2026-08-11  
**Root status:** `OPEN_NO_SOLUTION_CERTIFICATE`

## Atom attacked

C017 removed every XOR-symmetric Hamming family from O9d10. The next obvious non-symmetric algebraic family is a coordinate-dependent bilinear relation, or more generally any graph whose adjacency matrix has low rank over `GF(2)`.

C018 refutes that entire low-rank fiber at the graph-cover level.

## New upper-bound adversary

Let `M_G` be the `N x N` adjacency matrix of a square bipartite graph `G`, interpreted over `GF(2)`, and let

`r = rank_GF(2)(M_G)`.

C018 proves, for every nonzero graph,

`rho(G,G_{N,N}) <= 3r - 2`.

The proof has two ingredients.

1. **Pullback monotonicity.** If a graph is obtained from a base graph by arbitrary left/right vertex maps, without requiring surjectivity, its full cover complexity cannot exceed that of the base graph.
2. **Rank factorization.** `M_G=UV` over `GF(2)` represents every edge bit as the inner product of an `r`-bit left feature vector and an `r`-bit right feature vector. Thus `G` is a pullback of the `r`-bit mod-2 inner-product graph. C012 already gives that base graph cover at most `3r-2`.

This is deliberately a graph-cover argument. It does **not** assume that the rank-factor feature maps are cheap Boolean functions of any chosen vertex encoding.

## Consequences

- Any family with `rank_GF(2)(M_G)=O(log N)` is dead as a super-logarithmic R004 target.
- Every pure bilinear graph `G_A(x,y)=[x^T A y = 1]` on `x,y in GF(2)^t` has rank at most `rank(A)<=t`, hence `rho<=3t-2=O(log N)` even when `A` is dense and non-symmetric.
- Low-dimensional parity-feature embeddings cannot evade C017 merely by preserving coordinate identity.

High `GF(2)` rank is only a necessary screen, not a lower bound on cover complexity.

## Residual C018-R1

O9d10 is refined to a stricter search lane. A viable next family must have provably super-logarithmic `GF(2)` adjacency rank **and** survive all earlier quotient, degree, arboricity, finite-state, population-count, product, cyclic/intersection, and pair-multiplexing upper-bound adversaries.

The next candidate fiber should therefore be explicitly non-bilinear/high-rank, with the rank lower bound checked before any graph-cover lower-bound narrative is attempted.
