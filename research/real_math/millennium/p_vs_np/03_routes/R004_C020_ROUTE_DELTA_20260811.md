## Result

C020 gives a proof-draft full-cover upper envelope

`rho(QR_p,G_{p,p}) <= D_intersection(QR_p | G_{p,p}) = O((log p)^3)`

for every odd prime `p`.

The construction is source-aligned with Cavalar–Oliveira's graph intersection model. Binary row/column predicates are free unions of stars. A dual-rail Boolean simulation maps each ordinary binary gate to at most one counted set intersection. Euler-criterion modular powering is implemented by `O(log p)` modular multiplications, each using `O((log p)^2)` Boolean gates via double-and-add, yielding the cubic-log envelope.

## Research consequence

The C019 Paley/quadratic-residue family remains admissible for a possible **super-logarithmic** cover lower bound, but it is no longer credible as a candidate for anything close to the random-graph `Theta(p)` cover scale. Algebraic high GF(2) rank, density, and lack of twins coexist with a direct polylogarithmic membership construction.

This is a useful adversarial lesson for candidate selection. Future high-rank families should be screened not only for quotient, degree, arboricity, finite-state, symmetry, and rank collapse, but also for low-cost arithmetic membership circuits before any lower-bound narrative begins.
