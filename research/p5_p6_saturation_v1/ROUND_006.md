## Route-family mapping

### Insight / representational change

Constraint-relaxation and chunk-decomposition work on insight problems supports a specific mechanism for impasse: the initial representation can impose constraints that are not required by the problem, and progress occurs when those constraints/chunks are relaxed or decomposed.

**Mapping to Orion:** already owned by `REPRESENTATION_OR_FIXATION_FAILURE`, representation lifting, and the rule that repeated same-basis failure reopens context/representation. No new method surface is needed.

**New activation detail:** repeated failure plus an inconsistency between the literal problem statement and the solver's current interpretation is a strong trigger for an explicit representation challenge. A representation challenger should list which current constraints are semantic necessities versus inherited/assumed encoding constraints.

### Self-explanation

Chi et al.'s self-explanation work links successful example learning to generating explanations that connect steps to principles rather than merely replaying worked solutions.

**Mapping to Orion:** already represented by the Self-RAKL `explanation reconstruction challenge`, mechanism ancestry, and method-transfer requirement to record *why a method works* rather than only the method name. No new surface.

**Activation detail:** when retrieval finds a successful analogue/tool but transfer preconditions are poorly understood, require reconstructing the enabling explanation before reuse rather than copying the action sequence.

### Productive failure

Productive-failure research separates generation/attempt phases from later consolidation/contrast and shows that failure can support learning under the right consolidation conditions.

**Mapping to Orion:** already represented by immutable negative history, residual-driven candidate generation, contrastive parent comparison and post-result synthesis/consolidation. No new surface.

**Activation detail:** failure is not intrinsically productive. It earns learning value only when the failure generates a discriminating residual/contrast and is later consolidated against a correct/stronger model. This reinforces the current rule that a failed candidate alone cannot justify LIFT or a new method.

### Deliberate practice / competence frontier

Deliberate-practice work emphasizes targeted practice at weaknesses with informative feedback rather than undirected repetition.

**Mapping to Orion:** already represented in the Self-RAKL human-breakthrough candidate architecture by `competence-frontier deliberate-practice scheduler`, and in the training extension by learner-conditioned structural coordinates. No new surface.

**Activation detail:** practice allocation must be driven by a verified weakness/near-frontier coordinate and accompanied by feedback that can update the relevant skill model; volume alone is not a learning mechanism.

### Retrieval practice

Retrieval-practice research shows that actively retrieving stored information can improve later retention more than repeated exposure in tested educational settings.

**Mapping to Orion:** already represented by retrieval-rehearsal / transfer benchmarking, reconstructable memory views and reuse testing. No new surface.

**Activation detail:** if a durable ResearchTool/Lesson is expected to support long-horizon reuse, evaluate retrieval/rehydration success and transfer after delay/context change rather than assuming storage implies usable memory.
