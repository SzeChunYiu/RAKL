## Main methodological insight

A top-tier RAKL paper should not be forced to depend on a profitable strategy. The real scientific application is upstream:

\[
\text{scientific description}
\to
\text{predictive distribution}
\to
\text{downstream decision transform}.
\]

This makes the experiment valid even when downstream trading economics are null or unavailable. Profitability is not a substitute for scientific model validation.

## Self-evolution integration

The quant repository supplies unusually strong real hidden-defect families for self-evolution experiments. These include leakage, null misspecification, missing replication, multiplicity, estimand mismatch and chronology/clock overreach.

However, historical knowledge of these defects creates contamination risk. Benchmark construction must withhold the defect label and reserve genuinely fresh assurance tasks. Replaying a known bug with its name exposed is only a unit test of diagnosis, not evidence of autonomous weakness discovery.
