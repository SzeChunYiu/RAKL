# V2 parent-boundary addendum

The `ATMS_PROV_REVISION` architecture in the v2 development benchmark is a diagnostic abstraction, not a strongest-fair-parent superiority result. General truth-maintenance, provenance, belief-revision and argumentation systems can represent application-specific propositions and rules, including scientific authority coordinates if configured to do so. Therefore the v2 representation comparison must not claim that typed RAKL authority is more expressive than those general formalisms.

Paper I's positive mechanism target is instead causal ablation of its scientific authority contract: full typed basis versus leave-one-coordinate-out, scalar authority, pairwise-only gluing, proposal/workspace/experience authority leakage, missing negative-history retention and missing CANNOT_CHECK. An equal-information generic rule engine is an expressiveness oracle: if programmed with the same transition law it may match the full system, which would confirm that the contribution is the scientific governance contract, typed state and enforced noninterference rather than generic rule-engine expressiveness.

The 0.6786 ATMS+PROV+revision development bound is retained only as a diagnostic of what a richer parent abstraction already explains.
