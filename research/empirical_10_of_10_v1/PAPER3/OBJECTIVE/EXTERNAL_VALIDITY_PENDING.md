Before the strongest Paper II claim, retain separate evidence coordinates for:

1. a strong current semantic reranker/cross-encoder;
2. a direct LLM transfer-validity judge under matched context and budget;
3. a mechanism-alignment LLM comparator;
4. disjoint downstream routing utility;
5. natural-domain independent-human validation, if obtainable.

AI-generated labels are not substitutes for independent-human labels.
