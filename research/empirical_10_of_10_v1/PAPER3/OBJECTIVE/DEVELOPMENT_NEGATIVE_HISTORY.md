# Paper II objective Track A — preserved development negative history

This file records an intentionally failed development design. It is not confirmatory evidence and grants no scientific authority.

## Failed design

The first known-world generator produced invalid items by directly perturbing one named structural coordinate, and the structural extractor exposed essentially the same coordinate. On the ordinary development set the full structural rule appeared excellent, but the preregistered **coordinate-ablated twin** removed the directly perturbed coordinate from the extractor and the apparent advantage collapsed toward chance with a very high invalid false-accept rate.

That result means the original design largely measured the generator's own construction rather than a residual transfer-validity signal. Per `research/paper3/PAPER3_TRACK_A_REGISTRATION_V1.md`, it was rejected as a confirmatory instrument rather than repaired after confirmatory outcome access.

## Repair performed before confirmatory freeze

The successor development design:

1. assigns truth only by executing a family-specific verifier on the target world;
2. tests that mutating hidden perturbation metadata cannot change the verifier outcome;
3. adds independently derived target-effect obligations that are not the generator's named perturbation field;
4. adds valid-near and invalid-distant controls so surface similarity cannot identify the answer;
5. requires the lexical baseline to remain inside the preregistered 0.35–0.75 development difficulty band;
6. reruns the coordinate-ablated twin before freezing confirmatory size or seed.

The successor development receipt is `DEVELOPMENT_RESULT_V1.json`. The twin remains imperfect (as required for an informative falsifier) but retains substantial information beyond the lexical baseline after the directly perturbed coordinate is removed.
