# Paper-boundary map after question saturation

The purpose of this map is anti-salami and anti-claim-leakage: one result should have one natural paper owner unless there is an explicit cross-paper dependency.

## Paper III — Method-Evolution Mechanics

Owns the **general calculus**:

```text
What is a mechanic?
How is experience converted into a method candidate?
What separates proposal, deployment, evidence and method authority?
What fresh-assurance/governance transition licenses installation?
How can deterministic/LLM-free executors participate?
```

Paper III does not need to demonstrate that the entire Orion engine beats external research agents. Its current abstract already correctly states that end-to-end fresh-task improvement remains capability-blocked.

## Paper IV — Structural Learning Mechanics

Owns one **learning-state specialization**:

```text
Does typed structural learner state carry incremental information about what exposure is valuable next?
Can an allocation policy turn that information into fresh transfer/cost benefit under hard harms?
Does the same exact structural identity remain useful at inference?
```

Paper IV should not become a generic method-evolution/self-improvement paper. Training allocation is a specific consumer of the RAKL structural substrate.

## Paper V — Verified Discovery in Mathematics

Owns one **high-assurance application/promotion specialization**:

```text
What evidence product is required before a machine-generated mathematical result enters the research record?
How are intended specification, theorem truth, novelty, value and verifier trust kept noncompensatory?
What safety/recall/cost does that promotion contract produce?
```

Proof-search algorithms and VTG are supporting proposal/search mechanics. A proof-search performance result does not own the research-promotion claim.

## Paper VI — Orion capstone

Owns the **closed-loop recursive demonstration**:

```text
fixed audited evaluator epoch
-> measure incumbent vs internal/external frontier
-> diagnose a real weakness
-> discriminator if necessary
-> mutate the smallest justified method layer
-> development
-> fresh assurance under unchanged evaluator
-> validity-gated frontier update
```

And, for higher-order changes:

```text
evaluator/meta-policy mutation
-> close current epoch
-> outer-assured evaluator transition
-> anchor replay / drift measurement
-> new evaluator epoch
```

Paper VI therefore demonstrates Paper III's calculus operating recursively across a whole system; it does not redefine the calculus.

## Cross-paper claims

### IV -> III

A structurally useful training policy becomes a method candidate. Its installation is governed by Paper III semantics.

### IV -> VI

Paper VI may report Paper-IV state/policy as one component in the engine, but a capstone win cannot retroactively prove Paper-IV's causal residual.

### V -> III

A proof/search/assurance mechanic can itself be learned or revised, but method authority remains distinct from theorem authority.

### V -> VI

Verified mathematics supplies a domain with hard oracles and an explicit trust surface. It is a strong self-evolution testbed but must not become the only evidence for scientific-agent generality.

### III -> VI

Paper III supplies the semantics of governed revision. Paper VI supplies the external/fresh evidence that the assembled engine can actually execute a recursive improvement cycle.

## Anti-overlap rules

1. Paper IV does not claim generic self-evolution.
2. Paper V does not claim generic theorem-proving superiority unless its search lane independently earns it.
3. Paper VI does not repeat all component theorems/results; it cites component receipts and measures integration/self-evolution.
4. Paper III does not use a Paper-VI system win as proof that every method-evolution mechanic works.
5. One external-agent comparison belongs primarily to Paper VI; Paper III may cite it only as evidence for the governed method-evolution instance it actually tests.
6. Evaluator-governance theory can be stated in Paper III, but the epoch-level external recursive experiment belongs to Paper VI.
