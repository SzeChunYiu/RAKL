**Purpose:** identify what is already occupied and force every RAKL claim onto an executable residual. This is a research map, not a legal patent search and not a guarantee that no unlisted work exists.

## Novelty verdict by candidate

### Candidate A — “QoI-conditioned neural embedding”

**Verdict: NOT NOVEL AS A STANDALONE CLAIM.**

Conditional Similarity Networks already demonstrate condition-selected similarity subspaces. The correct RAKL claim must add executable TCSQ sufficiency/protected-coordinate semantics and compare against a faithful conditional-metric baseline.

### Candidate B — “Relational/structural channel inside a transformer”

**Verdict: NOT NOVEL AS A STANDALONE CLAIM.**

Abstractors, relational bottlenecks, TransNAR, causal-abstraction/IIT and related neuro-symbolic work occupy this space. A RAKL dual stream is implementation architecture, not the contribution, unless exact RAKL applicability/witness semantics yield a residual.

### Candidate C — “Train on structural coordinates”

**Verdict: NOT NOVEL AS A STANDALONE CLAIM.**

Skill graphs, taxonomy-guided data synthesis, structural axes and concept/causal supervision already exist.

### Candidate D — “Use the same structure for training and inference”

**Verdict: TOO BROAD / OCCUPIED.**

SciForma now directly uses one structural inventory for training and inference-time editing. The RAKL residual must be exact reuse of directional QoI-/boundary-scoped transfer identities, not merely one inventory.

### Candidate E — “External agent discovers improvements and compiles them into weights”

**Verdict: TOO BROAD / OCCUPIED.**

DGM/Hyperagents cover self-improvement; LatentSkill covers text-skill→LoRA compilation; ILWS covers external auditable instruction improvement and later distillation. RAKL must demonstrate the value of its governed structural failure/authority mechanism.

### Candidate F — TCSQ as a validated problem/QoI quotient

**Verdict: POTENTIALLY HIGH NOVELTY; CURRENTLY STRONGEST.**

The residual is the conjunction of:

- immutable original problem;
- explicit QoI/context-conditioned erasure;
- protected/forbidden-loss ledger;
- sufficiency obligations and fail-closed validation;
- intervention/falsifier evidence;
- quotient-side solve followed by reconstruction;
- original-problem verification before promotion;
- representation change does not escalate authority.

SQ-1 and SQ-2 establish controlled pieces. SQ-3 must show net solver benefit against Context Codec/compression, formal slicing/state abstraction, mechanism-only abstraction and incumbent RAKL.

### Candidate G — Neural TCSQ + directional witness alignment

**Verdict: POTENTIALLY HIGH NOVELTY ONLY AS A CONJUNCTION.**

The neural claim should be:

> A model can learn a representation whose task-conditioned invariances are constrained by a validated TCSQ contract and whose cross-domain transport is constrained by directional RAKL witnesses, reducing invalid transfer or structural-OOD cost beyond strong conditional-metric, relational-bottleneck and causal-abstraction parents.

The current CPU experiment establishes only feasibility and does not establish the parent residual.

### Candidate H — Exact external↔training↔inference RAKL identity reuse

**Verdict: POTENTIALLY HIGH NOVELTY, UNTESTED.**

Strongest test: freeze one structural catalog before learner outcomes; train/probe/allocate using exact IDs and schemas; then on a *separate fresh inference corpus* reuse exactly those IDs/roles/relations/invariants/boundaries through `StructuralWitness`. Compare against semantic retrieval, latent/skill parent, ID-only ablation and a separately learned representation. If inference needs an unrelated representation, report two mechanisms rather than a shared substrate.

### Candidate I — Epistemically governed cognitive compilation

**Verdict: POTENTIALLY HIGH NOVELTY AS GOVERNANCE/LEARNING SYSTEM; UNTESTED.**

The distinctive object is not self-improvement but a typed transition:

```text
verified external residual
-> diagnosis with evidence identity
-> bounded training change class
-> checkpoint/probe/catalog-bound challenger
-> fresh disjoint assurance
-> promote/reject/narrow
```

with an invariant that the training projection can alter `theta` but cannot alter scientific evidence roots or authority by itself.

## Falsification discipline

The following outcomes **narrow or kill** the strong RAKL claim rather than being re-labelled as success:

- conditional metric learning matches Neural TCSQ on fresh structural OOD;
- IIT/relational bottleneck matches witness-aligned RAKL after matched information/compute;
- TCSQ total overhead erases quotient solver savings;
- adaptive RAKL does not beat static RAKL with adequate power;
- a generic model-aware parent matches adaptive RAKL;
- exact training structural identities add no inference-time value;
- a separately learned latent representation is required for inference success;
- external→weight compilation improves benchmarks but the RAKL diagnosis/governance layer adds no residual;
- any hard retention, boundary, hostile-near-miss or negative-transfer ceiling is crossed.
