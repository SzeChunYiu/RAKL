# RAKL Core Principles

1. **Object before answer.** Define what is being studied before comparing claims.
2. **Projection before competition.** A source is first treated as a contextual projection on an object facet, not a whole-object rival.
3. **Context before contradiction.** Align population, scale, observation, assumptions, units, and intervention before declaring claims inconsistent.
4. **Atomic recursion.** Every unresolved transformation may become its own knowledge fiber.
5. **Normalize before multiplying.** Map equivalent representations and terminology before expanding the global combination space.
6. **Compatibility before testing.** Prune mathematically, causally, observationally, or operationally impossible combinations before empirical search.
7. **Representation is not mechanism.** Predictive success may identify a useful effective law without identifying its microscopic cause.
8. **Mechanism requires ancestry.** A mechanistic claim must connect building blocks, interactions, coarse variables, effective terms, and downstream consequences.
9. **Formal invention must be externalized.** A certifying invented theory must be represented as typed symbols, equations/operators, mechanism structure, assumptions/regimes, falsifiers and lineage rather than remaining only natural-language reasoning.
10. **Residuals are information.** Failure should reopen the fiber dimensions capable of generating that residual.
11. **Failed candidates do not satisfy a positive goal.** When a lane has a frozen positive-goal contract, a failed candidate is retained evidence and a trigger for further invention; it is not successful project closure.
12. **Persistence never licenses fabrication.** A mandatory positive objective cannot justify invented evidence, target leakage, selective deletion, hidden multiplicity, or post-result threshold rescue.
13. **Discriminate rather than decorate.** Prefer experiments that make surviving hypotheses disagree.
14. **Known-answer and hidden-answer worlds first.** Validators and invention operators must succeed, fail, and return cannot-check in controlled or withheld-target worlds before native capability claims are trusted.
15. **Preserve negative history.** A refutation or null is evidence. A successor is a new versioned object.
16. **Exact over local where needed.** Audit bounds, linearisations, inversions, and proxy identities against the actual operating region.
17. **LLM proposes, evidence disposes.** The model can generate ideas, mathematical structures and syntheses but cannot promote them by confidence alone.
18. **Candidate identity before evaluation.** An invented formalism, its thresholds, predictions and falsifiers must be frozen before the evidence used to certify that candidate is exposed.
19. **Blind critique is different from reflection.** Same-context self-critique is useful but not independent review.
20. **Raw is not canonical.** Search discoveries and candidate mappings enter raw storage before promotion.
21. **Semantic novelty, not paper count.** Saturation measures retained new knowledge after deduplication.
22. **Knowledge saturation is not positive-goal closure.** Exhausting the current literature or model menu does not prove that a registered positive objective has been achieved or disproved.
23. **RAKL applies to RAKL.** Every method step, including the invention-operator basis, may be challenged by better practices under frozen meta-evaluation.
