# RAKL Core Principles

1. **Object before answer.** Define what is being studied before comparing claims.
2. **Projection before competition.** A source is first treated as a contextual projection on an object facet, not a whole-object rival.
3. **Context before contradiction.** Align population, scale, observation, assumptions, units, and intervention before declaring claims inconsistent.
4. **Atomic recursion.** Every unresolved transformation may become its own knowledge fiber.
5. **Normalize before multiplying.** Map equivalent representations and terminology before expanding the global combination space.
6. **Compatibility before testing.** Prune mathematically, causally, observationally, or operationally impossible combinations before empirical search.
7. **Representation is not mechanism.** Predictive success may identify a useful effective law without identifying its microscopic cause.
8. **Mechanism requires ancestry.** A mechanistic claim must connect building blocks, interactions, coarse variables, effective terms, and downstream consequences.
9. **Residuals are information.** Failure should reopen the fiber dimensions capable of generating that residual.
10. **Discriminate rather than decorate.** Prefer experiments that make surviving hypotheses disagree.
11. **Known-answer worlds first.** Validators must succeed, fail, and return cannot-check in controlled worlds before native evidence is trusted.
12. **Preserve negative history.** A refutation or null is evidence. A successor is a new versioned object.
13. **Exact over local where needed.** Audit bounds, linearisations, inversions, and proxy identities against the actual operating region.
14. **LLM proposes, evidence disposes.** The model can generate ideas and syntheses but cannot promote them by confidence alone.
15. **Blind critique is different from reflection.** Same-context self-critique is useful but not independent review.
16. **Raw is not canonical.** Search discoveries and candidate mappings enter raw storage before promotion.
17. **Semantic novelty, not paper count.** Saturation measures retained new knowledge after deduplication.
18. **RAKL applies to RAKL.** Every method step may be challenged by better practices under frozen meta-evaluation.
