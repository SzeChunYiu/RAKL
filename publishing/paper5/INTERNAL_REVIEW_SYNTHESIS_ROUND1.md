# Paper 5 internal review synthesis — post-review

**Boundary:** synthesis of three same-session adversarial passes. The passes were not mutually blind or independent and do not count as external peer review.

## Review emphases

1. AI systems / experimental design / causal attribution
2. formal methods / security / governance / trusted boundaries
3. novelty / significance / interdisciplinary readability

## Consensus strengths

- The central scientific question is clear: can longitudinal research experience improve the research method itself under a process that prevents self-certification?
- Paper 5 is distinct from Paper 4. Paper 4 governs mathematical claim authority; Paper 5 treats research-method evolution as the object of measurement and governance.
- The live RAKL_math programme is useful as a hostile longitudinal observatory when explicitly separated from confirmatory ground-truth benchmarking.
- The manuscript now distinguishes raw archive growth from retained typed semantic growth.
- The four-arm attribution design materially improves causal interpretability by separating total framework, static architecture, persistent experience and memory-content effects.
- The version-DAG / operator-override / post-promotion-attestation story is substantially more credible than treating every merge as evolution.
- The paper does not rely on a priority claim for episodic memory or self-modifying agents; related work is acknowledged and the combined contribution is narrower.

## Consensus blocking concerns for a strong empirical submission

### S-M1 — Prospective four-arm causal attribution has not been executed
Derived from R1-M1. This cannot be solved by manuscript editing. A strong empirical claim that RAKL helps the same model remains blocked until a frozen matched packet is run and all arm outcomes/resources/validity failures are published.

### S-M2 — Retained semantic novelty has not been independently audited
Derived from R1-M6. Internal seven-axis growth is currently valid as internal metrology only. A strong claim that the curve measures objectively new learned structure requires the preregistered external/sample audit.

## Consensus blocking concerns for a strong autonomous-promotion claim

These do not block the current paper because the manuscript explicitly scopes them as open hardening items, but they block a claim that RAKL already self-promotes safely:

- content-bound governance evidence replacing bare `governance_approved`;
- protected resolution of assurance/evaluator-separation/evidence-lineage facts rather than caller Booleans;
- promotion-grade certificate binding for local gluing verification;
- state/schema rollback enforcement for persistent learned state.

## Resolved architecture/protocol blockers in this review cycle

- attribution runs now bind exact before/after arm state and detect transfer-state leakage;
- sham-memory construction has a frozen-policy requirement and leakage/structural-match controls;
- confirmatory task count/strata/power approximation/multiplicity/stopping rules are preregistered;
- confirmatory tasks require independently checkable/held-out evaluators rather than Millennium root self-scoring;
- retained-novelty audit protocol is defined;
- metrology reports explicit measurement scope and can include exact legacy KnowledgeFibers;
- comparisons across different measurement universes fail closed;
- process costs carry a `cost_policy_id` and mixed units are marked non-comparable;
- telemetry schema surface names are tested against canonical `method_specs.py` contracts;
- “lattice growth” terminology is corrected to typed retained structured-state growth, with local lattice views only where mathematically defined;
- manuscript now contains a compact implementation/proposal/prospective status table;
- figure plan forbids illustrative fake result curves before data;
- operator override is explicitly non-validating;
- same-session reviewer roles are explicitly non-independent.

**YES.** The architecture, case-study framing, metrology, causal design and upgrade protocol are sufficiently coherent to continue as the Paper 5 research programme.

**NO.** It is still a draft/proposal-only challenger. Exact-head CI, current-main synchronization, governance review and a decision about which metrology components are Class-A instrumentation versus Class-B method changes are still required.

**YES, with scope.** No additional same-session prose-only blocker remains. The next high-value work is exact-head validation, prospective telemetry accumulation, benchmark packet finalization, and later external/independent review rather than another unconstrained rewrite.
