# RSHEA Paper V–VI promotion policy

Status: campaign control note. This document does not grant scientific or method authority.

## Negative is a recursion trigger, not a forced stop

A negative mechanic result is retained as immutable evidence. Unless the registered scope is closed by a verified impossibility/lower-bound result, the next RSHEA step is residual diagnosis followed by an alternative-generation pass before another successor is implemented.

Allowed alternative classes:

- `REUSE_OR_PARENT_ABSORPTION` — adopt the strongest existing method when it explains the residual;
- `REPRESENTATION_CHANGE` — change state/problem coordinates so the obstruction becomes tractable;
- `SELECTOR_OR_PORTFOLIO` — use different mechanics in different regimes rather than forcing one global winner;
- `LOCAL_LAZY_OR_CACHED_VARIANT` — preserve the useful mechanism while removing build/verification/reuse overhead;
- `ACTIVE_INTERVENTION` — change the observation channel when passive evidence is non-identifying;
- `COMPOSITION_OR_GLUE` — combine independently validated partial mechanisms under an interface contract;
- `NEW_MECHANISM` — invent only after the preceding routes are bounded and exhausted.

## Saturation rule

`SATURATED` is not a paper-count threshold. Before claiming saturation for an active mechanic, repeat search from materially different vocabularies and method families until no new load-bearing parent, mechanism, representation, counterexample, lower bound, cost coordinate, or falsifier is retained.

At minimum consider, when structurally applicable:

- strongest historical and current same-domain algorithms;
- algorithm selection / portfolios / per-instance selection;
- rational metareasoning and value-of-computation;
- abstraction, refinement, bisimulation and quotienting;
- active information acquisition / experimental design;
- dynamic and incremental search;
- partial-order reduction / concurrency reduction;
- caching, reuse, memoization and invalidation;
- proof planning, premise retrieval and decomposition;
- verification/checkpoint scheduling;
- cross-domain obstruction→transformation analogues.

If a materially different search adds any retained object above, the lane is `NOT_SATURATED` and the new object must be accounted for before the next implementation.

A bounded saturation claim must record the searched universe/query families and a coverage receipt. Retrieval failure or budget exhaustion is not saturation.

## Promotion-grade terminals

A mechanic lane may close only as one of:

- `PROMOTE_TO_MECHANIC` — fully supported in the registered scope;
- `PROMOTE_CONDITIONALLY` — machine-readable supported/negative/boundary regimes with baseline fallback for negative/unknown states;
- formal/production conformance at its explicitly limited authority boundary;
- verified impossibility/lower-bound closure of the registered hypothesis;
- explicit retirement/supersession by a stronger parent/successor, preserving negative history.

A negative candidate alone does not satisfy a positive-goal lane, and persistent search never licenses evidence fabrication, evaluator weakening, post-outcome threshold rescue, or retroactive preregistration.
