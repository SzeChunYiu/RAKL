# Paper module: From component comparison to governed method assimilation

## Motivation

Adjacent LLM-science systems should not be compared only as monolithic pipelines. A framework may be strong in retrieval, another in formal checking, another in experiment search, and another in long-horizon memory. RAKL therefore treats each external system as a source of local method projections over atomic research fibers.

## Evidence-governed assimilation

For an external atomic operator `m`, define the contract

\[
C_m=(I_m,O_m,\gamma_m,A_m,P_m,F_m,\alpha_m^+,\alpha_m^-,T_m,B_m),
\]

where `I/O` are interfaces, `gamma` is context, `A` assumptions, `P` provenance/dependencies, `F` failure modes, `alpha+` authority the operator may mint, `alpha-` authority it must not mint, `T` the transition map into the target RAKL fiber, and `B` the frozen benchmark identity.

The lifecycle is:

```text
DISCOVER
-> DECOMPOSE
-> NORMALIZE
-> DEDUPLICATE
-> DEAUTHORIZE UNSUPPORTED SCOPE
-> MAP
-> SHADOW TEST
-> NARROW PROMOTION / PARALLEL VIEW / REJECTION
```

The deauthorization step is central. Retrieval does not mint claim support, prediction does not mint mechanism, formal proof does not mint empirical truth of its premises, and multi-agent agreement does not mint independent evidence.

## Non-forced method fusion

The Knowledge Atlas principle applies recursively to research methods themselves. If two operators are compatible under a registered scope, an explicit transition map may allow composition. If they are semantically equivalent, RAKL deduplicates them. If they depend on incompatible assumptions, both may remain local method views rather than being forced into one universal workflow.

This is a direct method-level analogue of obstruction-preserving scientific synthesis.

## Novelty boundary

Typed skills, validated skill libraries, executable skill programs, dynamic tool retrieval, skill/tool co-evolution, provenance and anti-pattern memory all have current prior art. The paper must credit SkillOps, SkillFoundry, HASP, SkillSmith, S1-NexusAgent, Scientific Agent Skills and related modular-agent traditions.

The candidate RAKL contribution is not modularity itself. It is the conjunction of contextual method charts, explicit semantic transition witnesses, scientific authority envelopes, negative-history preservation, obstruction-preserving non-fusion, semantic deduplication and the existing frozen self-promotion boundary.

The concurrent Round-012 similarity work also motivates a future refinement: transition witnesses should eventually declare admissible mapping families and distinguishing probes so semantic adaptation cannot be made arbitrarily flexible after observing a desired import. That is a preregistered residual, not a result of the present benchmark.

## Preregistered comparison

The paper should compare three matched conditions:

1. uncontrolled aggregation of attractive external operators;
2. a fixed human-curated pipeline;
3. RAKL contextual assimilation with typed authority and transition contracts.

Primary registered metrics are cross-axis authority leakage, false method duplication, invalid transition acceptance, forced-fusion error, negative-history resurrection and decision-relevant value per cost.

A null result is informative. If the simpler fixed or untyped pipeline matches RAKL on these failure modes under matched budgets, the assimilation contract remains explanatory architecture rather than a demonstrated empirical contribution.
