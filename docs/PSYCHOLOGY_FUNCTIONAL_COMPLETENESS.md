# Psychology-Informed Functional Completeness of RAKL

Status: functional architecture map, not a claim of biological brain simulation.

## Principle

RAKL does not need to imitate human feelings or neural anatomy.  It needs the research-control functions that make an experienced scientist learn from challenge, notice error, change strategy, preserve useful experience, and transfer what was learned.

The question is therefore not `does RAKL have emotion X?` but:

> Does a human cognitive/learning function expose a missing computational control that materially changes scientific research behavior?

## What is intentionally not simulated

RAKL currently has no need to simulate subjective emotion, personality, ego, fatigue sensations, or biological reward chemistry.  Their useful research functions are represented through resource budgets, risk/value portfolios, error signals, learning progress, persistence/switching and help-seeking controls.

If a future real-project residual shows that an omitted psychological construct predicts a distinct research failure not expressible through these functions, it becomes a new method-basis/ontology residual and must be benchmarked before adoption.

## Remaining psychology-adjacent empirical gaps

After Challenge Learning Control, no obvious high-impact *functional category* is missing for launching a controlled real project.  Several categories remain empirically open:

1. whether learning-progress-based challenge selection beats simpler priority rules;
2. whether RAKL attributes the cause of hidden failures correctly rather than merely noticing them;
3. whether adaptive help seeking improves transfer per cost;
4. whether experience really consolidates into reusable procedural skill across projects;
5. whether strategy switching occurs at the right time rather than too early or too late;
6. whether learned skills survive environment/model/domain change;
7. whether cross-model and cross-domain transfer survives fresh assurance;
8. whether social/independent review adds non-redundant evidence beyond same-context reflection.

These are now evaluation questions for the real-project phase rather than reasons to keep expanding the conceptual architecture indefinitely.
