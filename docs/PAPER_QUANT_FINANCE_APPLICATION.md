# RAKL Paper — Quant-Finance Application

Status: publication integration plan; research-only; no change to active RAKL behavior.
Date: 2026-08-09

## 1. Chosen real scientific case

The prospective real-science case for the RAKL methods paper is the existing `SzeChunYiu/polymarket_crypto` research programme.

The application is **spot-first**. The primary scientific object is short-horizon cryptocurrency spot movement. The application has two headline outputs:

1. a descriptive multiscale spot-movement framework;
2. a predictive 5m/15m spot-path model.

The validated spot law may later be projected through oracle and contract semantics into a Polymarket probability/application layer, but Polymarket information does not define the primary spot model and cannot repair a failed spot claim.

## 2. Why this is a suitable RAKL test

The quant programme is not a toy benchmark. It contains the kinds of epistemic problems RAKL is designed to govern:

```text
source and schema authority
multiple clocks and partial causal order
cross-venue/cross-asset context
representation-versus-mechanism ambiguity
heavy tails and nonstationarity
hidden state and partial identification
model-family multiplicity
transport across days/assets/venues
negative and null results
measurement and target mismatch
long research lineage
resource/context constraints
```

It also contains real historical failure modes that can be reconstructed into hidden-defect self-evolution tasks without inventing artificial favorable examples.

The two manuscripts may cite the same immutable experiment receipts but must not maintain conflicting active interpretations of one result.

## 5. Self-evolution experiment embedded in the quant project

Use historical real failure classes as development tasks while withholding the defect label. Candidate classes include:

- test-target leakage through nuisance estimation;
- wrong correspondence null;
- missing file-disjoint replication;
- multiplicity across a frozen horizon family;
- descriptive AC(1)^2 confused with direct nested OOS-R2 attribution;
- within-file physical ordering confused with cross-file chronology.

A positive self-evolution claim requires the repaired method to transfer to a fresh assurance task not used to design the change.

The paper should show failed/meta-overfit generations rather than reporting only successful descendants.

## 6. Spot descriptive result

The application should produce a contextual descriptive state over at least:

\[
D^{spot}=(R,V,J,A,M,L,G,X,O),
\]

where the coordinates represent return/displacement, volatility, tails/jumps, activity/duration, memory/reversal, local microstructure/liquidity, global state, cross-asset/venue dependence and observation/clock state.

Every retained object is separately typed as descriptive, predictive, mechanistic, identified/set-identified, refuted, blocked or cannot-check.

## 7. Spot predictive result

The primary headline horizons are 5 and 15 minutes. The predictive object is a distribution/path packet, not only an up/down label.

The existing spot-bridge estimand remains:

\[
\Delta_{joint}=\min(R_D,R_G)-R_{DG},
\]

with a preregistered material threshold and multiplicity-aware lower confidence bound on untouched/forward data.

Use strong micro-only, global-only, tabular, event-history, sequence/deep, full-history-teacher and compact-state challengers where resource matching permits. A simpler parent winning is a legitimate result.

## 8. Downstream Polymarket transform

A valid spot path can be mapped downstream:

\[
P_t^S(ds)
\to K_O(do\mid s)
\to Y=\Psi(o)
\to q_t
\to \text{PM-native comparison}.
\]

Each arrow is its own evidence/transition contract. A correct spot predictor does not imply oracle identity, PM incremental value, fill value, profitability or trade authority.

## 9. Publication authority boundary

Until the prospective experiments are executed, the methods paper may say:

> We preregister a real quant-finance application in which RAKL is used to construct and validate a multiscale descriptive and predictive model of cryptocurrency spot movement.

It may not yet say:

> RAKL improves scientific discovery in quant finance.

That sentence requires the matched workflow experiment.

Likewise, `self-evolving` remains a candidate capability until fresh-assurance method generations are observed.
