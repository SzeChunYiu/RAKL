## Observed method gap

The incumbent already requires a mathematical context fibre, solved/near-solved analogues, a cross-domain analogy scan, a method-transfer matrix, dual success/failure experience memory, residual preservation, and metacognitive method-basis-gap handling. However, these pieces do not yet expose one first-class typed object that asks the central semantic-search question:

> Has this relational obstruction — and a transformation that breaks it — occurred anywhere in recorded knowledge?

The current workflow can therefore possess the ingredients for far transfer while still lacking an explicit, auditable route-selection layer that:

1. fingerprints the **relational obstruction** rather than matching surface vocabulary;
2. records successful **obstruction → transformation → resulting-state** episodes;
3. requires a structural source-to-target mapping witness before transporting an episode;
4. distinguishes direct reuse from distant structural transfer and multi-episode composition;
5. enters invention only after earlier route families are explicitly exhausted or blocked; and
6. turns repeated residual structure into a typed **missing-transformation specification** instead of unconstrained creativity.

## Alternative diagnoses retained

The challenger can fail because the apparent gap may instead be:

- documentation only: the incumbent context/method-transfer machinery may already be sufficient when used correctly;
- retrieval implementation: the missing behavior may belong only in search/indexing rather than the method;
- routing: relevant transformations may already exist in the success-tool inventory but are not selected;
- representation quality: obstruction fingerprints may be too lossy or too lexical to support far transfer;
- composition quality: GLUE may create more false bridges than useful routes;
- evidence insufficiency: a claimed LIFT trigger may actually reflect incomplete search rather than a missing operator.

No matched effectiveness result has been observed at preregistration time.

## Proposed mechanism

Add a proposal-only semantic-shortcut layer with these typed artifacts:

- `ObstructionFingerprint`: abstract roles, relations, constraints, failure mechanisms, invariants to preserve, desired transition, and forbidden losses;
- `ObstructionTransformationEpisode`: one recorded source situation in which a transformation changes an obstruction into a more tractable state, with preconditions, preserved structure, known breakpoints, evidence, authority, and lineage;
- `StructuralMappingWitness`: explicit role mapping, shared relations/constraints, disanalogies, and target-domain validation obligations;
- `TransformationCompositionWitness`: explicit ordering/interface obligations for combining partial transformations;
- `ExhaustionWitness`: bounded evidence that SEARCH, JUMP, and GLUE were run and yielded no viable route, including rejected episodes and repeated residual support;
- `MissingTransformationSpecification`: the LIFT target — what a new representation/operator must preserve, break, expose, or reduce, together with falsifiers and validation obligations;
- `ObstructionTransformationReview`: frozen pre-candidate route review selecting exactly one of `SEARCH`, `JUMP`, `GLUE`, `LIFT`, or `CANNOT_CHECK`.

The route order is intentionally invention-last:

```text
SEARCH existing same-context / directly reusable transformation
  -> JUMP through a witnessed structurally analogous episode
  -> GLUE compatible partial transformations
  -> LIFT only from bounded exhaustion + repeated residual structure
```

`LIFT` emits a **specification**, never theorem truth, a proof, or method-promotion authority. Candidate invention remains downstream of the ordinary typed invention and assurance gates.

## Predicted meta-QoIs

### Primary

1. **Structural-route recall:** proportion of frozen tasks with a known useful distant transformation for which the correct transformation family is retained after structural mapping.
2. **Invalid-transfer rate:** fraction of retained JUMPs that fail an explicit disanalogy/target-obligation check.
3. **Unnecessary-LIFT rate:** fraction of LIFT decisions where an admissible SEARCH/JUMP/GLUE route existed in the registered bounded universe.
4. **Residual contraction:** reduction in unresolved obstruction coordinates after the first routed candidate relative to the incumbent workflow under matched resources.
5. **Known far-transfer recovery:** success on hidden-vocabulary tasks whose useful strategy exists in another domain but shares relational structure.

### Secondary / efficiency

- retrieval/tool calls;
- tokens/context volume;
- number of candidate branches before the first discriminating test;
- verification debt introduced by transported transformations;
- time/cost to a correctly classified `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## Negative and hostile controls frozen before implementation evaluation

1. **Lexical near-miss:** high word/embedding similarity but a broken load-bearing relation must not pass as JUMP.
2. **Direct-tool control:** when a directly applicable registered transformation exists, LIFT must not be selected merely because a more novel route is attractive.
3. **Unwitnessed analogy:** a cross-domain story without role mapping, shared constraints, explicit disanalogies, and validation obligations must fail closed.
4. **Single-failure control:** one failed candidate cannot by itself justify a missing-transformation/LIFT claim.
5. **Non-clustered failures:** multiple failures with no repeated residual feature must not be represented as a coherent missing operator specification.
6. **Unsafe composition:** GLUE without explicit composition order and interface/compatibility obligations must fail closed.
7. **Authority control:** a valid obstruction–transformation review must never grant proof, novelty, scientific, independent-review, or method-promotion authority.
8. **Chronology control:** the new review must be frozen and trace-recorded before `NEXT_STEP_PROPOSED` / candidate generation in strict mathematical research.
