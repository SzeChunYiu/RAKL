## Evidence boundary

Paper 5 is complete as a methods/protocol paper with retrospective/live case evidence and a preregistered prospective evaluation. It intentionally leaves future empirical outcome slots unfilled. A null, harmful or mixed attribution result remains a valid scientific result and must not be rewritten into a success narrative.
