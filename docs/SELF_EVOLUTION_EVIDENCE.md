# Evidence for Governed RAKL Self-Evolution

Status: publication/support theory. This document does not amend the Constitution, alter promotion, or claim that RAKL has already demonstrated open-ended empirical self-improvement.

## 1. Why "self-evolving" needs an evidence definition

A method variant that improves the benchmark used to design it has shown **local optimization**, not necessarily evolution in a scientifically meaningful sense.

Current automated-agent design and self-improvement work already demonstrates automatic invention/recombination of agentic systems, recursive self-modification, evaluator-driven program evolution, self-evolving scientific skill libraries, procedural ability-transfer evaluation and co-evolving agents/evaluators. RAKL therefore must not claim novelty from the bare fact that it can edit itself, accumulate skills, or combine agent modules.

The paper's stronger question is:

> **What evidence licenses the claim that an evidence-governed scientific method has improved itself rather than merely overfit its own evaluation loop?**

## 2. Two coupled learning channels

RAKL has two sources of candidate method change.

### Endogenous self-challenge

The current method discovers a residual and proposes a challenger to one of its own atomic fibers.

### Exogenous method assimilation

An external framework is decomposed into atomic operators and treated as local evidence about the meta-object:

> how should this scientific-research operation be performed?

The operator passes through:

```text
external mechanism
-> atomic decomposition
-> normalization/deduplication
-> authority envelope
-> transition witness
-> shadow eligibility
-> frozen evaluation
-> narrow promotion / parallel view / rejection
```

The two channels converge on the **same governed evaluation path**. External reputation and internal authorship provide no promotion authority.

## 3. Local improvement versus scoped evolution

Let method version `M_t` produce a challenger `M_{t+1}` on frozen development packet `D_t`.

A positive development delta

\[
\Delta_D(M_{t+1},M_t)>0
\]

with blocking invariants clean is **local improvement**.

Let `A_t` be a separately declared assurance packet that was frozen before the mutation, hidden from the proposer during optimization, and evaluated through a separate/protected evaluator.

RAKL records **scoped evolution evidence** only when:

\[
\Delta_D>0,\qquad \Delta_A>0,
\]

and blocking validity, exact candidate identity, history preservation, assurance chronology, evaluator separation and resource comparability are clean.

The claim is always scoped to parent/child versions, task distributions, models, tools, resource envelopes, evidence cutoff and evaluator identity. There is intentionally no global state called `EVOLVED`.

## 4. Meta-overfitting is a first-class negative result

If a challenger improves the development packet but regresses on registered assurance metrics, RAKL records:

```text
META_OVERFIT
```

The negative result remains in immutable method history.

## 5. Assurance capacity is consumable

A supposedly held-out benchmark stops being strongly independent when the optimizer repeatedly observes its score.

RAKL therefore treats a blind assurance set as a bounded **assurance reserve**. For packet `A` with preregistered optimizer-visible exposure budget `b_A`, fresh assurance requires prior exposure count `e_A(t) < b_A`.

After the reserve is consumed, the same packet may still show observed transfer but cannot independently certify unlimited later generations. A fresh/rotated assurance packet is required for another strong evolution claim.

## 6. Evidence-lineage independence is a newly opened child question

The current main branch now contains explicit evidence-lineage dependence machinery. This reveals a stronger future requirement: development and assurance tasks can be process-separated yet still descend from the same datasets, sources, generated cases, upstream benchmark or derivation chain.

Round 030 does **not** silently add this requirement because the self-evolution benchmark was frozen before the residual appeared. It opens:

```text
META_N094_ASSURANCE_LINEAGE_INDEPENDENCE
```

for a separately frozen experiment.

## 7. Method capability frontier rather than one super-agent

RAKL should not assume that assimilating more methods means concatenating them into one globally dominant workflow.

For atomic fiber `f` and context `gamma`, let the validated candidate set be

\[
\mathcal M_{f,\gamma}^{(t)}.
\]

RAKL should maintain a **context-indexed non-dominated frontier**

\[
\mathcal F_{f,\gamma}^{(t)} \subseteq \mathcal M_{f,\gamma}^{(t)}.
\]

A new internal or external method can therefore:

```text
DOMINATE_INCUMBENT
ADD_FRONTIER_POINT
EQUIVALENT
PARALLEL_LOCAL_VIEW
BLOCK / REJECT
CANNOT_CHECK
```

This is the rigorous interpretation of "absorbing strengths": **expand the validated capability frontier without forcing incompatible strengths into one monolithic agent.**

This frontier is theory-only here and cannot alter routing until a separate frozen benchmark validates a runtime implementation.

## 8. Stronger top-tier paper experiment

A headline Self-RAKL experiment should run multiple generations with three planes:

```text
DEVELOPMENT
  repeatedly available to proposer/evolution loop

TRANSFER
  held-out tasks used to diagnose generalization

ASSURANCE
  fresh/blind reserve used sparingly for publication-level evolution claims
```

Compare fixed RAKL, unconstrained self-editing, development-only benchmark evolution, governed RAKL self-evolution, and governed RAKL self-evolution plus external method assimilation.

For every generation report method diff, challenger source (internal/external), fiber, development delta, transfer delta, assurance exposure state, blocking invariants, cost and method-frontier disposition.

## 9. Strong falsifiers

The paper's self-evolution claim is weakened or refuted if:

1. gains disappear on blind held-out assurance tasks;
2. repeated assurance reuse explains apparent improvement;
3. improvements require evaluator mutation or weakened falsifiers;
4. preregistered cross-task/model transfer fails;
5. method growth increases cost without epistemic benefit;
6. uncontrolled aggregation performs as well as governed assimilation;
7. negative-history safeguards do not reduce resurrection of failed method ideas;
8. a prior method is semantically equivalent to the full governed evolution protocol.

## 10. Publication wording

Until prospective multi-generation transfer experiments exist, use:

> **RAKL implements a governed mechanism for recursive method evolution and external method assimilation; its ability to produce transferable multi-generation improvement is a preregistered empirical claim, not yet assumed.**

If the experiments succeed, stronger wording becomes defensible:

> **RAKL can recursively improve its scientific-research policy while preserving evidence safeguards, and can assimilate validated operators from external frameworks into a context-indexed method frontier.**
