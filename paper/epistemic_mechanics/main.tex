\begin{abstract}
Large-language-model research systems can generate hypotheses, write code, search literature and coordinate experiments, but these capabilities do not by themselves determine what a generated claim is allowed to change in a persistent scientific record. We develop an ``epistemic mechanics'' view in which scientific reasoning is represented as controlled transition over an external typed state. Claims are indexed by context, evidence, uncertainty and provenance; compatibility is mediated by explicit alignment maps; scientific authority is multi-coordinate rather than scalar; and proposal-generating models possess no direct canonical write authority. This state discipline is related to, but not reducible to, belief revision, truth-maintenance systems, abstract argumentation, database provenance or sheaf-theoretic local-to-global reasoning.

Two extensions address failures that arise even when canonical state is protected. First, a transient capacity-bounded workspace selects and broadcasts computationally useful material while remaining formally distinct from cross-context coherence and epistemic authority. We formulate the reference gate as a constrained top-$k$ problem, prove an optimality result for its reservation-first rule under the implemented assumptions, and separate computational influence from evidential provenance. Second, Open-World Mechanism Discovery (OWMD) begins from unowned functions rather than framework terminology. It constructs function signatures, executes heterogeneous retrieval routes, requires at least one lexically independent route, records citation/freshness obligations and permits only bounded discovery closure. We prove that unrestricted open-world completeness cannot be certified from any finite transcript without a complete membership oracle.

The project-level stopping rule is \emph{bounded epistemic saturation}: research continues while a new mechanism, derivation, independent evidence root, counterexample, negative result, novelty boundary, scope correction, unresolved fiber or discovery route still changes the registered state. A release requires repeated zero substantive gain under a frozen basis, stable discovery and nearest-work audits, a current freshness horizon and an expansion/consolidation order perturbation that leaves all substantive growth coordinates unchanged. In a bounded world this is a fixed-point construction. More generally, when an extensive, monotone and idempotent closure operator is declared, its closed epistemic states form a complete lattice with intersection as meet and closure-of-union as join. The current atom--witness--path implementation remains a typed compatibility complex unless those stronger obligations are supplied.

We give a three-context parity counterexample showing why pairwise compatibility need not imply higher-order gluing, prove that incomparable authority states cannot be faithfully collapsed into a single total-order scalar, and derive the exact simple-pendulum period and its finite-amplitude expansion as a worked mechanics trace. The resulting paper is a description of a bounded-saturated evidence-governed scientific state---a fixed point relative to an explicit research world, not a claim that science or the open literature is globally complete. It is likewise not evidence of phenomenal consciousness, completed independent peer review or empirical scientific superiority.
\end{abstract}
