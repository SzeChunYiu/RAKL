\begin{abstract}
Research systems accumulate evidence not only about scientific claims but also about the methods used to produce them. We formulate method evolution as an executor-independent reflective calculus. A RAKL mechanic is a typed state transition with declared preconditions, effects, invariants, costs, falsifiers and provenance; its executor may be a human, symbolic program, theorem prover, LLM or hybrid. Orion, the reference substrate, preserves immutable episodes and negative history, routes SEARCH/JUMP/GLUE/LIFT proposals through fail-closed audits, and separates proposal generation, deployment, performance evidence and method authority. Current conformance experiments establish structured verified experience-to-action transitions under fresh independently implemented oracles; end-to-end fresh-task improvement remains capability-blocked. We position reflection against rewriting logic, executable semantics, proof assistants, proof-carrying code, counterexample-guided refinement and proof-governed self-modification. LLMs are useful adapters and proposal generators, but no LLM output can self-certify a scientific or method update. We further define an LLM-free reference lane (RAKL-0) for mechanics that can be executed deterministically once inputs are formalized. The result is a scoped architecture for evidence-governed self-revision, not a theorem of globally optimal self-improvement.
\end{abstract}

Research agents, coding agents and language models were used as proposal, implementation, literature-search and drafting tools. They are not represented as independent reviewers, human experts or authors. Public research traces are auditable decision records, not disclosures of hidden model chain-of-thought.
