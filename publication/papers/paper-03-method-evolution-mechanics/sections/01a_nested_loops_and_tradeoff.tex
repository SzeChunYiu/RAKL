\section{Self-RAKL as two nested loops, and the question this paper now answers}
\label{sec:nested-loops}

This paper's subject is recursive solver improvement under evidence governance, and its
headline question is deliberately not ``does governed recursive improvement run''
(process existence is weak evidence) and not ``does the loop beat a static method''
(a sign-only headline invites exactly the scalarization this framework forbids). The
question is:

\begin{center}
\emph{What does evidence governance buy, and what does it cost, in recursive solver
improvement?}
\end{center}

\noindent It is answered as a measured tradeoff over a vector of outcomes ---
development throughput and solve-rate against false promotion, evaluator integrity and
honest-terminal rate --- and never collapsed to a scalar verdict, consistent with the
no-scalarization discipline inherited from the epistemic mechanics.

\paragraph{The two nested loops.}
Self-RAKL organizes recursive improvement as two loops:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{LOCAL loop} (per problem family): a problem failure enters typed RAKL
diagnosis; diagnosis attributes the failure to one stage; a candidate \emph{solver
mutation} is generated against that attribution; the mutation is validated on fresh,
gate-protected evidence; the outcome --- positive, negative, or refusal --- is
preserved. The architecture and governed-upgrade sections (through
\S\ref{sec:upgrade}) specify the machinery; Section~\ref{sec:structural-cautionary}
and Section~\ref{sec:tradeoff-results} report one complete executed cycle.
\item \textbf{GLOBAL loop} (across problem families): many local mutation trajectories
are themselves reduced into a structure space; structural analysis of that space
surfaces reusable mutation patterns and incompatibilities; a new solver generation is
constructed from the surfaced patterns; and the new generation is validated on fresh
cross-family evidence. Of these four stages, \emph{only the first two have been
executed} --- a first structural pass over nine receipted trajectories
(Section~\ref{sec:global-pass}) --- and the construct-new-generation and
cross-family-validation stages are prospective and unexecuted. No generational
improvement is claimed.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Relation to the structural-learning lane (former Paper~IV).}
By operator decision the structural-learning lane is combined into this paper: its
adaptive-training experiment is \emph{one mutation family inside the local loop}
(Section~\ref{sec:structural-cautionary}), and its instrument-admissibility mechanic
(oracle ceiling versus registered minimum detectable effect) is presented as a
\emph{discovered reusable mutation pattern} --- a global-loop output --- rather than a
standalone metrology paper. The lane's artifacts remain byte-intact in the research
record. This combination is exactly the cross-paper route that decision gate \#462
prescribed when it routed the lane \texttt{CONCEPTUAL\_CROSS\_PAPER\_ONLY}; the
standalone positive Paper~IV remains unauthorized and this paper does not revisit that
decision.

\paragraph{The Goodhart form of the question.}
The sharpest form of the headline question is this: everyone building self-evolving
systems \emph{knows} Goodhart's law --- that an optimized proxy stops measuring what
it proxied. If knowing is enough, governance is overhead. The thesis this paper
states and tests on its own receipts is that Goodhart-knowledge is protective
\emph{only when compiled into structures outside the optimizer's write-access} ---
authority separation, decay audits run by processes that did not author the gate,
non-compensatory hard gates, external anchors, freeze-before-outcome --- and never
when held inside the loop as a consideration, where it becomes one more optimizable
target (meta-Goodhart). The dead-gates corpus of
Section~\ref{sec:dead-gates} is the measured form of the first half: every one of
those four evaluators was built by a process that \emph{had} registered thresholds
and preregistration discipline --- intention-level Goodhart-knowledge was present and
insufficient --- and every one was caught by an audit standing outside the
gate-authoring process. The governed-versus-ungoverned experiment of
Section~\ref{sec:tradeoff-experiment} is the measured form of the contrast: the
ungoverned greedy arm is self-evolution \emph{without} Goodhart-as-architecture, the
governed arm is self-evolution \emph{with} it, and the end-state falsifiability
battery on each arm's acceptance signal is the decay detector. In one line, at the
scope this programme's receipts support: \emph{Goodhart-knowledge is protective only
when spent on boundaries, never when spent on intentions.}

\paragraph{Measured evidence, and its honest scope.}
Everything quantitative below is read from frozen receipts: the dead-gates corpus
(four independent evaluator failures across three papers, every one passing its own
registered thresholds; Section~\ref{sec:tradeoff-results}), a completed local-loop
cycle through an admissibility-licensed instrument, a governed-versus-ungoverned
acceptance experiment over one frozen proposal stream with three of five frozen
predictions falsified and preserved, a measured governance \emph{cost} cell on an
external evaluation surface, and the first global-loop structural pass. All of it is
same-context analysis within one programme; none of it is independent review; and
every pattern claim is scoped ``observed in this programme,'' never a universal law.
