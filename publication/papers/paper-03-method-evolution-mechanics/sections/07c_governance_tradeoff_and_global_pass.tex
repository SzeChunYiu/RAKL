\section{Measured results: the governance tradeoff, a completed local-loop cycle, and the first global pass}
\label{sec:tradeoff-results}

Every number in this section is read from a frozen receipt in the accompanying
artifact; freezes preceded outcome access throughout, and falsified frozen predictions
are reported as falsified.

\subsection{The dead-gates corpus: evaluator decay, observed and receipted}
\label{sec:dead-gates}

Under this paper's headline question, four receipted findings previously treated as
methods material are measured evidence: they are receipted instances of \emph{evaluator
decay in a self-improving pipeline} --- the failure mode evidence governance exists to
prevent --- observed in our own programme and caught by the governance layer's own
audit machinery. Each gate was passing its registered thresholds when caught:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Six-family robustness gate} (Paper~II lane): registered gate passed 6/6
at $p=0.03125$, then shown structurally incapable of failing --- scrambling every
source and target text left all extracted coordinates unchanged $810/810$, and one arm
of the ``paired'' statistic carried variance $1.2\times10^{-37}$
(\path{research/paper2_six_family_audit_v1/}).
\item \textbf{Gold-equals-prediction identity} (this paper's earlier instrument): four
accuracy-shaped gate conditions were satisfied \emph{by construction} and could not
fail; the repaired successor uses an independently implemented declarative oracle and
post-freeze gold, and its per-condition black-box audit returns FALSIFIABLE throughout
(\path{research/paper3_gate_falsifiability_audit_v1/}).
\item \textbf{Fail-open applicability licensing}: \texttt{assess\_transfer\_v2} returned
LICENSED with zero satisfied obligations for structures sharing no roles, relations or
invariants; repaired to fail closed --- an empty obligation set is an absence of
evidence (\path{research/transport_failopen_repair_v1/},
\path{research/transport_gate_reaudit_v1/}).
\item \textbf{Serialization round-trip identity}: a controlled-witness extraction gate
reported $1.000$ exact decisions that were subsequently narrowed to
serialization-interface fidelity --- gold and prediction traverse the same
serialization, so the perfect score measured the round-trip, not the capability
(\path{research/paper2_controlled_witness_extraction_v1/},
narrowing recorded in \path{research/orion_claim_frontier_v1/CLAIM_FRONTIER.json}).
\end{itemize}

The claim is exactly this and no more: four independent evaluator-integrity failures
across three papers of one programme, every one invisible to its own registered
statistics, every one surfaced by falsifiability-style audit rather than by outcome
anomaly. This corpus motivates measuring evaluator integrity as a first-class axis of
the tradeoff below; it is not evidence about pipelines in general.

\paragraph{The Goodhart reading, stated as measured evidence.}
Each of these four gates was authored by a process that knew Goodhart's law and acted
on that knowledge at the intention level: registered thresholds, preregistration,
frozen protocols. Knowing was insufficient --- all four decayed anyway, silently,
while green. What caught them was in every case a structure \emph{outside the
gate-authoring process's write-access}: a black-box perturbation battery, an
independently implemented oracle with post-freeze gold, a fail-closed contract on
empty obligations, an interpretation audit against the serialization interface. This
is the receipted form of the paper's thesis (\S\ref{sec:nested-loops}):
intention-level Goodhart-knowledge fails; boundary-level compilation of that same
knowledge works. Observed in this programme; not a universal law.

\subsection{A completed local-loop cycle through a licensed instrument}
\label{sec:local-cycle}

Section~\ref{sec:structural-cautionary} reported the mutation family's failure and its
attributed cause, and adopted instrument admissibility prospectively. That requirement
has now been executed end to end, completing one full local-loop cycle:
\emph{failure} (adaptive-v1 lost to its static parent; preserved) $\to$
\emph{diagnosis} (guard-rail budget capture plus within-round concentration; the
instrument itself provably unable to reach its own $0.05$ gate) $\to$ \emph{mutation}
(a marginal-gain challenger: per-slot believed-marginal-gain water-filling with guard
rails demoted from budget-consuming targets to constraints; the production allocator
byte-untouched) $\to$ \emph{fresh validation} (a licensed successor instrument and a
single frozen assurance run).

The admissibility mechanic itself was validated before use: known-answer tests cover
every sealed-packet counterexample; a five-case fresh-assurance battery with verdicts
frozen \emph{before} the runner existed matched 5/5 (including an ADMISSIBLE licensed
by a constructive lower bound alone where no monotone relaxation exists, and the
frozen-$\kappa$ no-rescue case); a per-condition black-box falsifiability audit passed
with expected SENSITIVE/INSENSITIVE outcomes declared pre-execution; and a
same-system ablation showed the upper-bound component carries the mechanic (dropping
it flips the reference verdict to \CANNOTCHECK{} in 32/32 trials)
(\path{research/paper4_instrument_admissibility_v1/}).

The repaired instrument (world-divergent initial learner state, dynamics and budget
imported unchanged) was licensed ADMISSIBLE on its first configuration: constructive
ceiling $+0.0800\ \ge\ \kappa\cdot\mathrm{MDE}=0.06$, rigorous upper bound $+0.0974$.
The gate did fresh pre-outcome work here: the ceiling over the strongest scalar parent
is only $+0.0046$, so the inherited ``beat the scalar parent by $0.02$'' gate would
have been a second structurally unreachable gate; it was re-registered sign-only
before any arm outcome. On the frozen assurance seed, consumed once, the challenger
cleared every gate: $F-D=+0.0760$ $[0.0755,0.0764]$, positive in $6/6$ worlds, safest
arm on the hard-safety floor
(\path{research/paper4_marginal_gain_challenger_v1/ASSURANCE_RECEIPT.json}).

\paragraph{A falsified diagnostic, preserved.} The frozen prediction P6 stated the v1
policy would remain negative in a licensed instrument. It is \textbf{falsified}:
$E(\text{v1})-D=+0.0747$ there. The preserved parent negative was therefore
policy$\times$instrument-conditional, and the strongest supported claim is
adaptive-versus-static \emph{in licensed instruments}; the specific
marginal-gain-versus-level mechanism contrast is small ($F-E\approx+0.0014$) and
remains open. The parent negative, its terminal, and the earlier attribution
prediction P1 --- itself falsified ($35\%$ of the gap recovered against a
pre-registered $\ge 60\%$) --- all remain verbatim.

\subsection{The governed-versus-ungoverned tradeoff, measured}
\label{sec:tradeoff-experiment}

The registered ungoverned comparator
(\texttt{MEC-GREEDY\_ACCEPT\_ON\_HELD\_OUT\_SCALAR}, from the external-agent registry;
the acceptance rule of ungoverned self-improvement loops: keep a mutation iff a
held-out scalar improves) was executed against the governed pipeline over the
\emph{identical} frozen stream of 14 allocator mutation proposals, with the outcome
vector, false-promotion definition, budgets and five predictions frozen before any
outcome access (\path{research/paper3_governance_tradeoff_v1/}). The truth meter is a
licensed-instrument evaluation at a disjoint seed, outside both arms.

\paragraph{Three of five frozen predictions were falsified, and the surprise is the
result.} We predicted the greedy arm would false-promote (TP1), out-accept the
governed arm (TP2), and accumulate noise-driven or content-inert acceptances (TP3).
None of that happened. In this environment the ungoverned failure mode is
\emph{silent starvation and verdict inversion}: on the inadmissible instrument every
marginal-gain proposal measured $\approx-0.001$ and was rejected --- $10$ of the $13$
rejected proposals clear the $0.05$ screen on the licensed instrument --- so the
greedy loop discarded the entire winning mechanism family, kept a candidate whose
measured value understates its true value $30$-fold, and emitted zero honest
terminals, because no refusal semantics exist for it to emit. Its final incumbent
nonetheless transfers positively (TP5, an honestly registered counter-prior): with
this proposal family, deficit-chasing policies are good, so the ungoverned loop was
lucky in outcome while blind in process. The false-promotion failure mode presumably
requires a noisier or looser environment; that regime was not exercised, and no claim
is made about it.

\paragraph{The decay-detector reading.} Under the Goodhart framing
(\S\ref{sec:nested-loops}) the arms are self-evolution \emph{without} and \emph{with}
Goodhart-as-architecture, and the end-state battery on each acceptance signal is the
decay detector. The predicted --- but deliberately not presumed --- signature was that
the ungoverned arm wins the raw development scalar while its scalar decays toward
non-falsifiability. The measurement came out differently, and both cells are reported
as the empirical answer: on the \emph{raw-gain axis} the ungoverned arm did not even
win by its own meter ($+0.0025$ against the governed $+0.0781$), because its
unqualified instrument could not express the available improvements at all; on the
\emph{evaluator-integrity axis} its signal's measured end-state failure is
expressiveness collapse --- verdict inversion on ten true positives and a $30\times$
understatement --- rather than optimizable-target capture, while the governed
acceptance signal remains FALSIFIABLE under the battery. Proxy capture and proxy
starvation are evidently two ends of the same defect --- an acceptance signal whose
relation to the target quantity has silently broken --- and which end manifests
depends on the instrument's ceiling relative to its noise, a regime distinction this
experiment exercised on one side only.

\paragraph{What governance measurably bought and cost here.} Bought: the licensed
comparison existing at all (instrument qualification), development/truth agreement,
refusal receipts on $14/14$ broken comparisons, and falsifiable end-state gates.
Cost: $+5$ eval-units ($\approx 36\%$) and a slower acceptance cadence --- plus, on a
separate external surface, a measured governance \emph{cost} cell that this paper
places on the frontier rather than burying: on the external STALE memory-mode
envelope, the typed governed surface scored $0.267$ against the naive ungoverned
arm's $0.483$ (ON$-$OFF $=-0.217$, CI $[-0.292,-0.133]$, $n=240$;
terminal \texttt{TYPED\_GOVERNANCE\_EXTERNAL\_SURFACE\_NOT\_SUPPORTED\_IN\_ENVELOPE}).
Two boundaries are load-bearing: the contrast is ON-versus-OFF governance only and is
never comparable to the published $55.2\%$ STALE ceiling; and the scoring rule treats
abstention like error --- the receipt's dimension structure (arms tied where both
commit; the typed arm \emph{below chance} exactly where the naive arm guesses at
chance) suggests refusals may be scored as failures, an evaluation-stage hypothesis
consistent with the dead-gates pattern, but the aggregate-only receipt cannot confirm
it, so a re-scoring experiment with abstention-aware rules is registered and frozen
(\path{research/paper3_stale_revival_v1/}) rather than executed. Until re-scored, the
cost cell stands at face value. No cell of this frontier is the paper's verdict; the
frontier is the result.

\subsection{The first global-loop structural pass}
\label{sec:global-pass}

The global loop's first two stages have been executed once: nine receipted local
trajectories --- the four dead gates, the instrument-ceiling trajectory, the
typed-refusal absorption, the STALE surface, the licensed challenger cycle, and the
tradeoff experiment --- were reduced mechanically into the structure space of
\path{src/rakl/structure_space.py} under a fixed role vocabulary
(\texttt{defect\_stage}, \texttt{superstage}, \texttt{lever\_type},
\texttt{instrument\_state}, \texttt{sign}), with every trajectory's receipt files
read at run time and one binding honestly recorded \CANNOTCHECK{} (a receipt that
lives on an unmerged branch). The space accumulated $34$ distinct roles with
per-round growth $[5,3,3,4,4,5,5,2,3]$ and is \textbf{OPEN} --- nowhere near
saturated (\path{research/paper3_global_structural_pass_v1/}).

Three patterns surface, each citing its trajectories, each scoped to this programme:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Evaluation-stage dominance.} Every trajectory whose defect stage is
confirmed lives on the evaluation side --- confirmatory gates, applicability
licensing, measurement interface, instrument power. Zero confirmed defects live on
the proposal-generation side. In this programme, the thing that decays in recursive
improvement is the \emph{measuring apparatus}, not the mutation generator.
\item \textbf{Healthy-signal inversion.} The broken instruments looked healthy by
their own statistics ($p=0.03125$; CIs ${\approx}0.0016$ wide; $1.000$ accuracy)
while structurally unable to measure their targets: health statistics and measurement
capacity are independent axes.
\item \textbf{Two lever families.} Every successful repair is refusal-shaped (make
the signal able to say no or nothing: fail-closed licensing, typed refusal, ceiling
admissibility, governed acceptance) or independence-shaped (make the signal
independent of what it judges: black-box audit, independent oracle with post-freeze
gold, interpretation narrowing).
\end{enumerate}

This is a first structural pass over nine trajectories --- explicitly \emph{not} a
validated generational improvement. The global loop's remaining stages (constructing
a next-generation solver from the surfaced patterns, and cross-family fresh
validation of that generation) are prospective, unexecuted, and carry registered
gates of their own before any claim can exist.

\subsection{Limits of the Goodhart claim}
\label{sec:goodhart-limits}

Three limits bound the thesis, and each is load-bearing. First, the decay detector
carries its own who-audits-the-auditor question; the answer here is grounding, not
recursion --- the battery's verdicts rest on external anchors (perturbations of
evidence, independently implemented oracles, post-freeze gold), and a battery audited
only by itself would reproduce the defect it hunts. Second, designer-level iteration
re-imports Goodhart at the meta-level: the humans and agents who choose protocols can
drift toward protocols that pass. The countermeasure exercised in this paper is
freeze-before-outcome plus preserved negatives, and the paper's \emph{own} falsified
predictions --- P1, P6, and three of the five tradeoff predictions, all reported as
falsified rather than absorbed --- are the working demonstration that the
countermeasure binds its authors. Third, no finite audit certifies
non-Goodhartedness; the open-world theorem applies. What the battery certifies is
bounded non-decay \emph{relative to the declared probes}, and a probe family that
nothing in the evidence can move would itself be the next dead gate.
