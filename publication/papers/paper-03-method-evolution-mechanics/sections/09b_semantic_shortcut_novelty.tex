\section{Novelty boundary for the semantic-shortcut loop}
\label{sec:semantic-shortcut-novelty}

The obstruction--transformation layer does not justify a broad priority claim. Classical derivational analogy and case-based planning already studied stored problem-solving experience, source--target correspondence, replay/adaptation, failures, repairs and partial-plan reuse; the internal prior-art record names Mostow (1989), Hammond (1990) and Veloso (1994) as representative parents. Modern LLM-agent work likewise establishes experiential learning, reusable workflow/skill memory and iterative feedback-driven refinement \cite{shinn2023reflexion,zhao2023expel,wang2024awm,wang2026autoskills,zhang2026memskill}. The bounded audit also checks recent structure-aware proof retrieval and counterexample/property-guided synthesis as close modern neighbors. These parent families anticipate substantial parts of the mechanism even when their terminology differs.

Accordingly, the claim available to Orion is narrower. The candidate contribution is the particular executable, fail-closed composition of:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item content-bound obstruction--transformation episodes with source authority, preconditions, effects, invariants, breakpoints and provenance;
\item vocabulary-light obstruction fingerprints for retrieval;
\item invention-last \texttt{SEARCH}$\rightarrow$\texttt{JUMP}$\rightarrow$\texttt{GLUE}$\rightarrow$\texttt{LIFT} routing;
\item explicit target structural/precondition witnesses and forbidden-loss checks;
\item complete-effect semantics for SEARCH/JUMP and partial-effect semantics for GLUE;
\item candidate-accounted, cross-problem-bounded exhaustion plus repeated residual structure before LIFT;
\item a LIFT output that is a missing-transformation specification rather than a self-authorized tool; and
\item continued separation between proposal/search memory and scientific, theorem, novelty or method-promotion authority.
\end{enumerate}

A bounded internal primary-source audit through 12 August 2026 did not identify one source containing this same complete contract. That result is not a global firstness certificate: terminology differs across planning, case-based reasoning, analogy, theorem proving, program synthesis and agent memory, and a combination of strong parent systems anticipates much of the conceptual space. The full source identities, component comparison and strongest-parent attack are frozen in the committed prior-art audit record. Independent external reviewers were unavailable and were not invented; issue \#403 closes under \texttt{INTERNAL\_PRIOR\_ART\_AUDIT} without minting independent novelty authority.

Paper 5 therefore claims an integrated method/governance design, not invention of analogy, experience memory, repair or compositional reuse in isolation. If a future independent external prior-art review finds one prior system that materially contains the complete contract, the novelty language should narrow further to an implementation/integration contribution without changing the empirical questions in this paper.

\paragraph{Re-statement against the reframed claim object (2026-08-14).}
The red ratings above were issued against the paper's \emph{old} signature
(promotion-scoring / admission-gating for a self-evolving method library). The
combined paper's claim object has changed (\S\ref{sec:nested-loops}): nested recursive
loops with evidence-bound refusal, plus a \emph{measured} governed-versus-ungoverned
tradeoff with evaluator-integrity as a first-class measured axis, plus mutation-space
structural analysis of receipted trajectories. Honest occupancy check, on the audit's
own record rather than fresh external review: CoEvoSkills occupies the self-evolving
verified-library \emph{architecture} and ConsistencyGate occupies write-time
\emph{admission scoring}; neither, on that record, reports a measured
governed-versus-ungoverned acceptance comparison with falsifiability auditing of the
acceptance signal itself, and neither analyzes its own mutation-trajectory space. If
either in fact contains such a measurement, the delta narrows to the specific
frontier axes measured here, and the matched-cost head-to-head obligation stated
above is unchanged either way. On the tradeoff question the closest neighbours are
different objects: the verification-gap survey of self-evolving agents
(arXiv:2608.05179), which reports that only a minority of surveyed systems fix seeds
or traces and that none carries an external in-loop oracle at its strongest level ---
a taxonomy of the gap, where this paper contributes a measurement inside it --- and
the ungoverned exemplars registered in the external-agent registry (the Karpathy
autoresearch greedy loop, from which the control arm's acceptance rule is taken
verbatim as \texttt{MEC-GREEDY\_ACCEPT\_ON\_HELD\_OUT\_SCALAR}, and Mimosa-style
LLM-judge fitness selection), which report throughput but do not measure false
promotion, honest-terminal rate or end-state evaluator integrity. The delta claimed
under the reframed question is therefore the tradeoff \emph{measurement} --- not the
existence of governance, gating, or self-improvement, each of which is occupied.
