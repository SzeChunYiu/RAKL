\subsection{Closest-parent novelty boundary (MemTX, PPMF, AutoSci, MemClaw)}

A Paper-II novelty claim that compared only against weak or function-mismatched baselines would be false advertising. The closest-parent audit therefore decomposes candidate contributions into atomic functions and measures each against the strongest feasible primary-source parent \cite{memtx2026,ppmf2026,autosci2026,memclaw2026,riosgarcia2026}. Persistent scientific memory, workflow/lifecycle orchestration, DAG-shaped skill reuse, transactional write/commit, provenance-bearing memory, permissions, cascade repair/supersession, shared-memory governance and generic feedback-driven self-improvement are treated as \emph{inherited} mechanisms unless a full-text residual survives.

Under that discipline, MemTX already owns staged belief commit, provenance retention, supersession and cascade repair \cite{memtx2026}; PPMF already owns source-authority non-amplification at action time over a channel trust lattice \cite{ppmf2026}; AutoSci already owns two-tier scientific memory, harnessed literature-to-rebuttal lifecycle skills, experience-conditioned retrieval/templates and SciEvolve versioned updates to organization, skills and templates \cite{autosci2026}; MemClaw already owns governed shared-memory scope, provenance reconstruction and per-record temporal supersession in a live service \cite{memclaw2026}. Orion therefore does not claim novelty for those functions in isolation. Where MemTX is strictly stronger---notably transitive cascade repair---the correct response is adoption, not a residual claim.

What the audit currently retains as narrowed residuals is more specific: context/regime-scoped scientific claims; contradiction and negative-history preservation as scientific evidence rather than only rollback audit; partial identification as an admitted terminal state; multi-axis prediction/mechanism/identification authority typing beyond source-channel firewalls; experience that may improve search or skills without minting those authority coordinates; authority-axis transition audit beyond epistemic-operation graphs \cite{riosgarcia2026}; bounded freshness-expiring \emph{coverage} saturation as distinct from per-record expiry; and method evolution gated on held-out fresh assurance after a challenger exists. Each residual carries a falsifier in the machine-checked matrix.

The decisive \emph{empirical} novelty test for the typed-authority layer remains the matched contrast
\[
\texttt{A3\_TRANSACTIONAL\_GOVERNANCE}
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\texttt{A4\_SCIENTIFIC\_AUTHORITY\_TYPING},
\]
with Authority Leakage Rate and valid-upgrade recall reported together so that pure conservatism cannot masquerade as typing. Confirmatory identification of that contrast is blocked while \path{CAPABLE_MODEL_AVAILABLE}=\path{NO_REFUTED}. A prior non-confirmatory harvest (job 3476749) scored A3 with ALR $\approx 0.154$ and valid-upgrade recall $0$, and A4 with ALR $0$, valid-upgrade recall $0$ and false-conservative refusal $0.625$. That pattern is conservatism under a capability floor, not an A4$>$A3 novelty win, and it grants no scientific authority.
