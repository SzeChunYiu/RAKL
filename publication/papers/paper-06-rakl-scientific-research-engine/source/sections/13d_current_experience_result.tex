This result closes the basic two-arm empirical residual: the framework can now report what happened under a matched RESET/LEARNING execution rather than only preregistering the comparison.  Under the same publication evidence cutoff, the stronger novelty campaign is terminal on the negative/narrowing path: the learning-by-authority-governance factorial (\#155), closest-parent/function-matched ablations (\#156) and protected experience-to-method promotion experiment (\#157) are honestly blocked as \path{CANNOT_IDENTIFY} after the \#247 capability-floor receipts through 7B and the \#379 V2\_0\_EXEC sealed revisit (job 3476813; \path{MODEL_CAPABILITY_FLOOR_7B_V2_EXEC}); learning, CONTENT and confirmatory ALR claims remain unlicensed while \path{CAPABLE_MODEL_AVAILABLE}=\path{NO_REFUTED}.  The umbrella campaign (\#158) records negative/narrowing completion rather than pending positive empirics.  Independent external review (\#216) remains a separate human-open coordinate.  Paper II therefore treats the present negatives as constraints on the claim, not as a reason to redefine success post hoc, to lower the frozen $\ge 2/3$ ORACLE gate after outcomes, or to shop a larger model outside the frozen ladder.

A separate, independently frozen external-model comparator (reported in Paper~II's objective lane) bears on \emph{why} this blocker persists without lifting it.  On the objective transfer benchmark a frontier-class model clears the capability floor that the frozen Qwen ladder could not (three-way accuracy $\approx0.71$ versus the $0/3$ floor receipts through 7B), and driving that model through the fail-closed applicability gate cuts invalid-transfer false-accepts $0.53\!\to\!0.34$ with non-overlapping intervals while a compute-matched control moves nothing.  This is evidence that the master residual is a matter of \emph{model availability under governance}, not a defect of the mechanism: the gate produces a real system-level uplift once a model clears the floor.  It does \emph{not} license the learning, CONTENT or confirmatory ALR claims and it does \emph{not} flip \path{CAPABLE_MODEL_AVAILABLE}.  That model sits outside the frozen $0.5$B--$7$B ORACLE ladder, the comparator is a preliminary single-model/single-seed lane rather than a licensed ladder step, and promoting it here would be exactly the ``shop a larger model outside the frozen ladder'' move the preceding paragraph forbids.  Lifting the blocker still requires an authorized in-ladder capable-model execution (or an explicitly re-frozen ladder) together with the independent external review of \#216.
