\section{Unified problem-solving substrate: exact legality, partial maps and fallible geometry}
\label{sec:unified_problem_solving}

The preceding sections separate evidence, workspace, experience and semantic-shortcut routing. A complementary question is how Orion should represent the \emph{process of solving} a difficult problem without turning a routing heuristic into scientific authority. The implementation now isolates a proposal-only unified solver substrate whose central separation is
\[
\text{legal transformation semantics}
\;\neq\;
\text{materialized operational map}
\;\neq\;
\text{reachability geometry}
\;\neq\;
\text{navigation dynamics}
\;\neq\;
\text{epistemic authority}.
\]
The first coordinate says which candidate transitions a registered verifier would accept. The second records which transitions have actually been discovered, checked, refuted, left stale or remain unknown. The third is a derived, potentially learned and fallible estimate of goal-relative reachability. The fourth determines which candidate action receives compute. None of these routing objects changes the canonical scientific-authority projection without an ordinary evidence-bearing transition.

\paragraph{Operational map belief, edge assurance and coverage.}
The new \texttt{OperationalMapReceipt} distinguishes \texttt{VERIFIED\_TRANSITION}, \texttt{CANDIDATE\_UNVERIFIED}, scoped refutation, failed-but-not-refuted attempts, stale edges and \texttt{UNKNOWN}. The load-bearing rule is
\[
\texttt{UNKNOWN}\neq\texttt{BLOCKED}.
\]
A search failure in an incomplete map therefore cannot establish impossibility. Even a no-route result under certified complete registered coverage is explicitly scoped to the bound operator basis, chart and closure subject and grants no scientific or theorem authority. This closes a gap between a verifier's transition semantics and the partial cognitive map available to a research agent.

A second boundary matters for theorem proving: \emph{verified applicability} is not a state transition, and not all verified state transitions have the same assurance meaning. Orion distinguishes a kernel-derivation edge, which carries a locally checkable derivation receipt, from a replay-validated operational tactic edge, which is bound to the exact live proof-state snapshot, tool/version subject and replay receipt. Both may support search; only the ordinary final proof/certificate gate can grant theorem authority. Candidate operational edges remain non-traversable until the relevant assurance is established.

\paragraph{Paths, partial orders, abstraction and certificates.}
Canonicalizing states alone does not eliminate duplicate search when independent transformations commute. Orion therefore admits verifier-witnessed path-equivalence objects and partial-order traces as \emph{derived search views}. Raw TaskEpisode chronology remains immutable, and absent dependency metadata is never treated as proof of commutation. This also sharpens a second distinction:
\[
\text{discovery trajectory}\neq\text{solution certificate}.
\]
The implemented assembly gate binds the final dependency DAG to an exact content hash, requires the root dependency closure, verified dependencies, a verified root and an audited proof receipt bound to the certificate artifact, and then returns only \texttt{READY\_FOR\_EXTERNAL\_AUTHORITY\_GATE}. It cannot self-promote the result.

A semantic/QoI quotient is also not automatically a solver quotient. Exact reachability-preserving quotient claims require target preservation and suitable two-way simulation/lifting obligations. A sound over-approximating abstraction may intentionally admit spurious abstract routes; such a route must be concretized/replayed or trigger refinement. The abstract object itself never mints solution or impossibility authority.

\paragraph{Noncompensatory path cost.}
Solver paths carry incomparable costs: compute, verification, representation construction, evidence acquisition, risk, trust and new assumptions. Orion therefore applies hard legality, specification, portal, trust and root-scope constraints \emph{before} cost comparison. Only admissible paths enter a Pareto frontier or an explicitly registered consumer-specific ordering. The theorem-level path-cost contract records how each coordinate composes---for example sum, maximum, set union or a registered partial order---instead of silently adding unlike quantities. A cheap route cannot compensate for an unlicensed assumption or failed preservation contract.

\paragraph{Mechanic differential diagnosis and verified solver compilation.}
When progress stalls, the proposal-only Mechanic Differential Diagnosis (MDD) layer represents competing causes---for example map coverage, representation, scale, metric, dynamics, operator basis, verifier, interface, learned-geometry support/OOD, model/tool floor or compilation cost---and remains set-valued when one cause is not identified. A discriminator can be requested before repair. The Verified Solver Compilation (VSC) layer then binds a candidate representation, transformation, solver, decoder and verifier together with claimed transformation effects, preservation evidence, build/execute/decode/verification costs, expected reuse and invalidation risk. A compilation cannot be marked validated even for routing without a separate subject-bound preservation receipt.

\paragraph{Nontrivial geometry and learned-artifact lifecycle.}
A perfect local routing field is not automatically an improvement: exact target distance can be precomputed from a finite graph and then make greedy routing trivial. Orion therefore registers a geometry's construction/verification cost, coordinate or label size, auxiliary storage, local query cost, target dependence, gold-route/gold-distance access, expected reuse, invalidation hazard, route stretch and fresh-transfer scope. An evaluator-only oracle geometry can define an upper bound but cannot count as evidence for a nontrivial solver gain.

If a geometry is learned from previous search, its training subjects, behavior/search policy, sampling process, label source, coverage coordinates, unseen operators/charts, leakage tests, fresh subjects, OOD detector and staleness detector are explicit procedural provenance. This blocks a self-confirming loop in which the current policy avoids a region, produces no training data there and later interprets that absence as evidence that the region is poor. Reusable geometries/fields are procedural tools with build cost, break-even and invalidation semantics, not scientific facts.

\paragraph{Fibred multiscale Orion and global amalgamation.}
The resulting view is recursive but not assumed literally fractal. A child problem fibre may instantiate its own local map, representation, operators, geometry and verifier while remaining bound to a parent QoI and interface. Coarsening is a task-conditioned quotient, not permission to erase root-relevant distinctions. A child result returns through GLUE and parent verification; all locally verified children can still yield an emergent parent-level composition residual. A parent assembly therefore binds child certificate hashes, overlap/substitution consistency, discharged assumptions, parent invariants and a final parent verifier. Pairwise local success is not a substitute for global amalgamation.

\paragraph{Reachability quantifiers.}
The initial mathematical programme is deterministic, where existential path reachability is well defined. If future Orion mechanics include stochastic, nondeterministic or adversarial actions, the reachability claim must state whether it means existence of one path, controllability by a policy, robustness for all admissible outcomes, almost-sure reachability, a probability lower bound, an expected-cost bound or a game/adversary guarantee. These are different scientific objects; deterministic route language must not be reused silently.

\subsection{Development known-world stress tests and their limits}
The repository includes a deterministic generator \path{research/unified_problem_solving_v1/run_known_world_stress.py} and a publication script \path{paper/generate_unified_solver_figures.py}. These tests are deliberately small mechanism falsifiers. They do not estimate frontier scientific capability and grant no method-promotion authority.

One family makes field construction expensive but repeated extraction cheap. The exact break-even law is
\[
q > \frac{C_{\mathrm{build}}}{C_{\mathrm{baseline}}-C_{\mathrm{extract}}}
\]
when the denominator is positive; the plotted registered toy uses $C_{\mathrm{build}}=100$, $C_{\mathrm{baseline}}=30$ and $C_{\mathrm{extract}}=10$. Another family constructs a long route whose locally preferred action is correct on most decision points but whose last strict-greedy descent enters a dead end; bounded best-first search over the same heuristic retains an alternative branch and reaches the goal. Thus local geometric congruence is not by itself closed-loop navigability.

\subsection{Closure ledger and recursive assurance}
The same fail-closed discipline is applied to the framework review itself. The registered VTG closure ledger binds each currently known issue to a canonical Orion owner surface and one disposition: code/test closed, theorem/assumption bounded, scope-gated, open empirical, open external assurance, or rejected as an invalid claim. A closed software issue must identify executable tests and evidence; an open empirical issue must preserve a falsifier and next epistemic cut. The ledger is evaluated against the exact Git subject during CI. This borrows the useful review mechanic from the Nature-Skills hardening process---release-critical closure is provenance-bearing data rather than a bare Boolean---while retaining the explicit boundary that same-context review is not mutually blind external assurance.

The ledger can support the statement that every \emph{registered} issue is classified and owned; it can never prove that no hidden issue exists. New counterexamples, peer review or experiments reopen the relevant fibre rather than being reconciled away.

\paragraph{Claim boundary and next cut.}
The implemented sidecars and tests establish scoped software/architectural invariants; the known-world figures establish only the stated constructed mechanisms. They do \emph{not} establish that formal mathematical reachability has a useful hidden geometry, that MDD diagnoses real research failures accurately, or that VSC outperforms strong algorithm-selection/reformulation parents. For the mathematics lane, the next frozen question is therefore whether verifier-defined reachability admits a succinct, constructible, leakage-free and reusable directed reachability representation whose local queries reduce total verified proof-search cost on held-out theorem families after geometry construction, storage, routing and verification costs are accounted for. Paper~V states that preregistration explicitly.
