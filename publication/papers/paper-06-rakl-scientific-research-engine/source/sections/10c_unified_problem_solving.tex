\section{Unified problem-solving substrate: exact legality, partial maps and fallible geometry}
\label{sec:unified_problem_solving}

The preceding sections separate evidence, workspace, experience and semantic-shortcut routing. A complementary question is how Orion should represent the \emph{process of solving} a difficult problem without turning a routing heuristic into scientific authority. The implementation now isolates a proposal-only unified solver substrate whose central separation is
\begin{gather*}
\text{legal transformation semantics}
\;\neq\;
\text{materialized operational map}
\;\neq\;
\text{reachability geometry}\\
{}\neq\;
\text{navigation dynamics}
\;\neq\;
\text{epistemic authority}.
\end{gather*}
The first coordinate says which candidate transitions a registered verifier would accept. The second records which transitions have actually been discovered, checked, refuted, left stale or remain unknown. The third is a derived, potentially learned and fallible estimate of goal-relative reachability. The fourth determines which candidate action receives compute. None of these routing objects changes the canonical scientific-authority projection without an ordinary evidence-bearing transition.

\paragraph{Operational map belief and coverage.}
The new \texttt{OperationalMapReceipt} distinguishes \texttt{VERIFIED\_TRANSITION}, \texttt{CANDIDATE\_UNVERIFIED}, scoped refutation, failed-but-not-refuted attempts, stale edges and \texttt{UNKNOWN}. The load-bearing rule is
\[
\texttt{UNKNOWN}\neq\texttt{BLOCKED}.
\]
A search failure in an incomplete map therefore cannot establish impossibility. Even a no-route result under declared complete coverage is explicitly scoped to the registered operator basis, chart and coverage contract and grants no scientific or theorem authority. This closes a gap between a verifier's transition semantics and the partial cognitive map available to a research agent.

\paragraph{Paths, partial orders and certificates.}
Canonicalizing states alone does not eliminate duplicate search when independent transformations commute. Orion therefore admits verifier-witnessed path-equivalence objects and partial-order traces as \emph{derived search views}. Raw TaskEpisode chronology remains immutable. This also sharpens a second distinction:
\[
\text{discovery trajectory}\neq\text{solution certificate}.
\]
The implemented assembly gate binds the final dependency DAG to an exact content hash, requires the root dependency closure, verified dependencies, a verified root and an assembly-verifier pass, and then returns only \texttt{READY\_FOR\_EXTERNAL\_AUTHORITY\_GATE}. It cannot self-promote the result.

\paragraph{Noncompensatory path cost.}
Solver paths carry incomparable costs: compute, verification, representation construction, evidence acquisition, risk, trust and new assumptions. Orion therefore applies hard legality, specification, portal, trust and root-scope constraints \emph{before} cost comparison. Only admissible paths enter a Pareto frontier or an explicitly registered consumer-specific ordering. A cheap route cannot compensate for an unlicensed assumption or failed preservation contract.

\paragraph{Mechanic differential diagnosis and verified solver compilation.}
When progress stalls, the proposal-only Mechanic Differential Diagnosis (MDD) layer represents competing causes---for example map coverage, representation, scale, metric, dynamics, operator basis, verifier, interface, model/tool floor or compilation cost---and remains set-valued when one cause is not identified. A discriminator can be requested before repair. The Verified Solver Compilation (VSC) layer then binds a candidate representation, transformation, solver, decoder and verifier together with claimed transformation effects, preservation evidence, build/execute/decode/verification costs, expected reuse and invalidation risk. A compilation cannot be marked validated even for routing without a separate preservation receipt.

\paragraph{Fibred multiscale Orion.}
The resulting view is recursive but not assumed literally fractal. A child problem fibre may instantiate its own local map, representation, operators, geometry and verifier while remaining bound to a parent QoI and interface. Coarsening is a task-conditioned quotient, not permission to erase root-relevant distinctions. A child result returns through GLUE and parent verification; all locally verified children can still yield an emergent parent-level composition residual.

\subsection{Development known-world stress tests and their limits}
The repository includes a deterministic generator \path{research/unified_problem_solving_v1/run_known_world_stress.py} and a publication script \path{paper/generate_unified_solver_figures.py}. These tests are deliberately small mechanism falsifiers. They do not estimate frontier scientific capability and grant no method-promotion authority.

One family makes field construction expensive but repeated extraction cheap. Under the implemented Bernoulli invalidation model with per-query rebuild hazard $h$ (i.i.d.\ invalidation, rebuild cost $C_{\mathrm{build}}$), and under the stated idealizations of stationary per-query costs on a single commensurable scalar scale, the implemented break-even law is
\[
q > \frac{C_{\mathrm{build}}}{\,C_{\mathrm{baseline}}-C_{\mathrm{extract}}-h\,C_{\mathrm{build}}\,},
\]
valid only when the denominator is positive; otherwise the field never amortizes. The hazard-free special case $h=0$ recovers $q > C_{\mathrm{build}}/(C_{\mathrm{baseline}}-C_{\mathrm{extract}})$, which is what the plotted registered toy uses ($C_{\mathrm{build}}=100$, $C_{\mathrm{baseline}}=30$, $C_{\mathrm{extract}}=10$, $h=0$). This scalar law is a registered development projection, not the noncompensatory path-cost algebra that governs route admissibility. Another family constructs a long route whose locally preferred action is correct on most decision points but whose last strict-greedy descent enters a dead end; bounded best-first search over the same heuristic retains an alternative branch and reaches the goal. Thus local geometric congruence is not by itself closed-loop navigability.

\paragraph{Operational definition of the solvability field (cost/search semantics).}
Wherever this paper says a solver \emph{descends a field} or follows a \emph{solvability field}, it means the following concrete construction, not an algebraic-norm/trace or \'etale-descent mechanism. A solvability field is a scalar potential $\Phi:X\to\mathbb{R}$ defined over the solver's constructed state set $X$ at a fixed subject $\Theta$, whose sign is used only to rank candidate expansions in a best-first (or bounded-branch) search. ``Descending'' means selecting a successor $x'$ with $\Phi(x')\leq\Phi(x)$ before expanding higher-ranked alternatives; it does not assert that a geodesic, metric or differential structure exists unless that is separately certified. The field is a proposal-only routing object: $\Phi(x)$ can guide where compute is spent but cannot place $x$ in the verified solution region. It is charged at its full search cost, namely (i) a one-shot \emph{construction cost} $C_{\mathrm{build}}(\Phi)$ to build or learn the potential over $X$, plus (ii) a per-step \emph{extraction/sweep cost} $C_{\mathrm{extract}}$ to evaluate $\Phi$ at each expanded state, plus (iii) an \emph{invalidation/rebuild hazard} $h$ when the underlying map changes under the field (the break-even law above governs when such a field amortizes). A field whose construction+sweep cost exceeds its search reduction is, by construction, a net loss and must not be reported as an acceleration. In Figure~\ref{fig:unified_field_hypothesis} the potential is the obstacle-blind Manhattan cost-to-go, given rather than learned; the experiment tests whether even a given useful field pays for itself, not whether one can be constructed for arbitrary representations.

\subsection{Assurance sidecars, cross-stage identity and the unified integration contract}
\label{sec:unified_assurance_sidecars}

The unified substrate separates routing from authority by construction, but several components admit a caller-named receipt or a local validation object that could otherwise be mistaken for self-authenticating. A family of additive assurance sidecars closes these gaps without changing legacy content identities. Each is contract-only: it states fail-closed preconditions and grants no scientific, proof, transfer, model-promotion or governance authority. They are plumbing that makes an invariants layer machine-checkable, not evidence that the underlying research mechanics work.

\paragraph{The unified solver registry.}
The unified solver registry is a typed manifest of every solver-sidecar mechanic admitted to the substrate. Each entry binds a mechanic to its implementing module, an owner surface among the registered method surfaces, its test paths, an authority effect constrained to \texttt{NONE} or \texttt{PROPOSAL\_ONLY} (solver sidecars are forbidden to claim more), a purpose string, and its empirical-open coordinates. An entry that omits a test path, claims a stronger authority effect, or lacks an empirical-open coordinate is rejected. The registry is therefore the gate that prevents an unreviewed sidecar from inheriting the legitimacy of the surface list; registration is a coverage and discipline claim, not a capability claim.

\paragraph{Path congruence for quotient substitution.}
Verifier-witnessed path equivalence records that two adjacent independent transformations commute, but safe substitution of one subpath inside an arbitrary surrounding proof context requires more: the equivalence must be a congruence under composition. The path congruence module closes this gap with the free partially commutative monoid (Mazurkiewicz traces), the minimal parent structure under which same-trace equivalence is both an equivalence relation on all histories and a congruence $u\sim v\Rightarrow p\cdot u\cdot q\sim p\cdot v\cdot q$. Foata normal form supplies a canonical representative, so equivalence is decidable without permutation search. The load-bearing fail-closed rule is that the runtime commutation witnesses are state-indexed, certifying commutation at one state only; quotienting a real history therefore fails closed unless a separate, externally supplied global-independence certificate establishes that the declared pair commutes in every reachable state. The abstract monoid laws remain available for words over the declared relation; a trace-equivalence class is a bookkeeping object and grants no proof or theorem authority.

\paragraph{Mechanic-diagnosis state machine.}
Mechanic Differential Diagnosis represents competing causes set-valuedly; the diagnosis state machine governs how that set is refined. It is a strict immutable transition system: a cause may be ruled out only by an explicit discriminator/evidence transition, the candidate and ruled-out sets must be disjoint and unique, and a verdict of \texttt{NO\_GAP} requires both no surviving causes and bound evidence. Its central honesty rule is that a unique concrete cause cannot be hidden behind \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK}: when exactly one concrete cause remains the verdict must identify the gap rather than defer. The machine grants neither scientific nor method-promotion authority; it only prevents a diagnosis process from silently collapsing to a confident single cause or to an ambiguous stall.

\paragraph{Composable approximation budget.}
A quotient or portal may carry a local \texttt{VALID\_APPROXIMATE} receipt, but a chain of such receipts is not a license for arbitrary composition. The approximation budget module binds each certified error to a metric, a scope and a registered composition law (additive, max, or a separately registered custom law), and requires the accumulated bound to fit the registered decision budget. Assessment returns \texttt{WITHIN\_BUDGET}, \texttt{EXCEEDS\_BUDGET}, or \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK} when no steps are supplied; an exceeded budget cannot be hidden by averaging. It grants no scientific authority; an approximation that passes its budget is admissible for routing, not promoted to an exact result.

\paragraph{Semantic quotient assurance.}
The Task-Conditioned Structural Quotient module correctly separates a quotient proposal from its validation object, but a caller can still instantiate a passing validation report as a bare verdict plus evidence identifiers. The semantic quotient assurance sidecar makes that report load-bearing by binding it to a resolved verifier/replay receipt whose hashes match the proposal, source and report content exactly. It is additive: historical quotient hashes are unchanged, and a caller-named receipt identifier alone never suffices. The sidecar grants no scientific authority; it only refuses to treat an unbound validation object as production-grade.

\paragraph{Authority assurance bindings.}
Scientific and derived authority objects need assurance plumbing that cannot itself become an authority root. The authority assurance module binds a certificate to its content hash, an attestation subject hash, an evaluator artifact hash, the bound evidence content hashes, and a trust-backend class ranging from an internal release fixture to an external public key, optionally with a transparency log. It distinguishes provenance-reference-only bindings from exact-scope-preserving reverified bindings, and a caller-named revalidation receipt is explicitly not an authority root: the ordinary scientific-promotion path must still resolve it. By construction the scientific-authority grant is constantly false; production-grade trust-root status is a property of the backend class, not of the binding. This is the specific RAKL configuration of a separation that concurrent work also realizes: CogEEGAgent~\cite{hou2026cogeegagent} pairs a deterministic contract-validation harness with fail-closed, evidence-bound release. The authority assurance sidecar is RAKL"s content-hash- and trust-backend-bound instance of that separation, not a novel primitive.

\paragraph{Structural identity bridge.}
A structural object presented to external reasoning, to training, and to inference must be identifiable across stages without caller-named hashes drifting. The structural identity bridge derives a typed structural identity bundle from the actual object content: the structure identity and content hash, the quantity of interest, the context hash, the optional quotient view and witness (each present only with its matching hash), and the boundary-contract hash. The preferred construction surface digests the object as presented rather than trusting a caller-supplied digest, so cross-stage reuse is keyed to exact content. The bundle is an identity object; it grants no authority and makes no preservation claim, which remains the separate obligation of the structural-transfer use contract described in Paper~V. Cross-stage identity reuse is not unique to RAKL: Skill-to-LoRA~\cite{zhang2026skill2lora} binds one identity object across training and inference and reports that naive cross-skill sharing degrades performance. The bundle is therefore RAKL"s content-hash-bound, staleness-checked instance of cross-stage identity binding rather than a new capability, and it inherits the same warning that sharing an identity across stages without preservation checking is unsound.

\paragraph{The unified integration contract.}
The component modules intentionally retain typed semantics rather than being flattened into one mega-state. The unified integration contract is a single manifest that binds the exact versions permitted to participate in one experiment or integration epoch and checks the cross-surface invariants no individual module can see by itself: base-commit and state-commitment identities, structural-identity and operational-subject digests, and the optional external receipts for geometry learning, quotient validation, structural-transfer use, training assurance, shared-identity reuse, compilation assurance and the exact-base guard. Its sole affirmative verdict is \texttt{READY\_FOR\_INTEGRATION\_TEST}; readiness means only that identities and required external receipts are sufficiently bound to run the next experiment, and a missing or contradictory binding returns \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK} or \texttt{REJECT}. It grants no scientific, proof, transfer, model-promotion or governance authority.

\paragraph{Claim boundary and next cut.}
The sidecars and tests establish scoped software/architectural invariants; the known-world figures establish only the stated constructed mechanisms. A reader should not infer that every named contract is enforced on the live execution path. The wired-in core (the immutable authority projection and certificate-bound promotion, the typed router, replay-verified operational-map routing, and the path-equivalence and epistemic-noninterference invariants) is machine-enforced inside the runtime. The additive assurance sidecars---semantic-quotient assurance (\texttt{assured\_materialize\_validated\_quotient}), structural-transfer use (\texttt{assess\_transfer\_for\_use}), and the unified-integration manifest (\texttt{UnifiedMechanicsManifest})---are contract-defined, fail-closed and pass their own unit tests, but at this revision no production caller routes through them; their propositions therefore hold only under their stated caller obligations, which are not yet machine-enforced on the execution path. Wiring these gates into the live call graph is registered open work, not a completed enforcement claim. They do \emph{not} establish that formal mathematical reachability has a useful hidden geometry, that MDD diagnoses real research failures accurately, or that VSC outperforms strong algorithm-selection/reformulation parents. For the mathematics lane, the next frozen question is therefore whether verifier-defined reachability admits a nontrivially local navigable geometry on held-out theorem families after geometry-construction cost and hidden-route leakage are accounted for. Paper~V states that preregistration explicitly.
