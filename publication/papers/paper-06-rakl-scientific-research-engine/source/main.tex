\begin{abstract}
AI research agents can search literature, generate hypotheses and execute analyses, but workflow completion does not determine which statements are licensed to change a scientific record or which past task experiences are safe to reuse. We present Orion, an evidence-governed architecture that treats research as operation over a persistent, typed, recursively evolving external substrate. The underlying language model may remain weight-frozen while future behaviour changes through accumulated external state. Orion couples four loops: information to knowledge, problem to solution, experience to method, and Orion to better Orion. Immutable TaskEpisode records preserve observed research trajectories before interpretation; versioned Lessons separate reflection from reusable method authority; problem-conditioned fibres compile knowledge, tools, episodes, failures, strategies and warnings; local results require explicit interface agreement before global synthesis; and saturation is vector-valued across knowledge, operators, experience patterns, obstructions, relations, paths and meta-methods. An optional training-time extension is represented separately as a checkpoint-bound training projection rather than being conflated with the canonical external-state learning loop.

The current implementation makes the experience--authority boundary explicit on a real composed runtime surface. \texttt{RAKLV3State} carries an immutable \texttt{ScientificAuthorityProjection} containing content-bound claims, evidence roots and active/historical scientific-authority certificates. Public v3 transitions are classified by ownership, and only separately attested scientific promotion, revocation and supersession transitions may move the scientific-authority coordinate. The v1 state fingerprint remains byte-stable for historical experience benchmarks while an explicit v2 fingerprint includes scientific authority. Executable epistemic-noninterference tests and integration-path mutations attack experience-to-evidence, routing/reflection/workspace/self-evolution leakage, provenance-to-independence, prediction-to-mechanism, mechanism-to-identification and unattested authority changes. This is an architecture/integration invariant, not a model-level authority-leakage result or empirical superiority claim. The deployed mathematical-research path additionally binds a content-addressed obstruction--transformation memory and routes proposals in invention-last order through \texttt{SEARCH}, structurally witnessed \texttt{JUMP}, interface-checked \texttt{GLUE}, and residual/coverage-bound \texttt{LIFT}; this proposal router does not mint scientific, theorem or novelty authority.

We also report the first interpretable matched \texttt{RESET\_BASELINE} versus \texttt{LEARNING\_ENABLED} continual-experience result. After two preserved prompt-interface failures, a frozen v1.2 packet produced 12/12 schema-valid native records with the intended $S_0\rightarrow S_n$ chronology and uncontaminated fresh transfer. Under Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, both arms achieved zero registered successes on development and transfer, with zero development success-score lift and no promotable transfer gain. The later \#247 ORACLE scale staircase was authorized through 0.5B, 1.5B, 3B and 7B. Its preregistered scale terminus, job 3476788 at 7B, was parse-valid on $3/3$ cases but succeeded on only $1/3$ and classified \path{MODEL_CAPABILITY_FLOOR_7B}. A separately frozen sealed-task revisit at the same 7B scale (\#379, job 3476813) was parse-valid $5/5$ with success $2/5$ and mean score $0.500$, classifying \path{MODEL_CAPABILITY_FLOOR_7B_V2_EXEC}; \path{CAPABLE_MODEL_AVAILABLE} remained \path{NO_REFUTED}. No Phase-0 learning staircase, confirmatory Wave-2 model empirics or promotional lift followed, and no unregistered 14B/32B scale shopping is licensed by these failures. The earlier V4.2 microtrial likewise had only one evaluated seed/task unit; although both arms were scorable at $3/5$ versus $3/5$, the registered arm comparison was not estimable. These are scoped negative/process results, not evidence that persistent experience never helps under other tasks or models. The paper therefore contributes the architecture, formalized integration obligations and auditable negative history; the semantic-shortcut router and optional training projection remain method/architecture surfaces whose efficacy, safety and cost require separately registered experiments. Under publication series v2 this manuscript is the capstone research-engine paper: historical \path{paper2} experiment namespaces remain immutable evidence aliases even though the reader-facing publication identity is Paper~VI.
\end{abstract}
