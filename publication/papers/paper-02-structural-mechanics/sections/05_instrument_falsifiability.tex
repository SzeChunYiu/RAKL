\section{Instrument falsifiability: a battery, three refutations of our own instruments, and a refutation of the battery}
\label{sec:falsifiability}

A confirmatory gate that passes is informative only if it could have failed. This section develops that requirement into an executable battery for transfer instruments and reports its application to four instruments of this research programme---in chronological order of discovery, with every negative preserved verbatim. The first instrument \emph{passed every registered gate} and was then shown to be non-falsifiable. The second scored a perfect $1.0$ that turned out to measure serialization fidelity. The third was built to the battery, was demonstrably falsifiable, read its text, rejected its own negative controls---and still failed its error-diversity gate, isolating what it measures to template inversion. The fourth instrument is the battery itself: one of its own probes was shown to measure something other than what it was registered to measure, and was repaired under a two-sided known-answer test. We consider the battery, validated by these refutations and by its own, the paper's most reusable methodological result---and we take the fourth case to be the one that most establishes the point, since a battery that cannot fail its own probes is subject to exactly the critique it exists to make.

\subsection{The battery}
\label{sec:battery}

For a benchmark with a gold decision function, candidate arms and a registered paired statistic, the battery comprises:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.4em,style=nextline]
\item[A. Gold-arm distinctness] No comparison arm may \emph{be} the gold function. If it is, the ``paired'' statistic is one arm's absolute loss minus a constant, and one arm has no variance.
\item[B. Seed-failability] Re-run the frozen summary on arbitrary non-registered seeds. A registered gate that no seed can fail is a property of the generator, not a test; a probative registration must exhibit at least one failing seed.
\item[C. Trivial-arm floor] Run \texttt{always\_accept}, \texttt{always\_reject}, \texttt{always\_abstain}. Any figure a trivial arm attains (e.g.\ zero false-accepts from \texttt{always\_reject}) is worthless alone; only joint properties no trivial arm attains are reportable. Corollary: report false-accept and valid-transfer retention as a pair, always.
\item[D. Equal-$n$ shuffle null] Destroy only the task$\,\leftrightarrow\,$coordinate binding while preserving marginals and the merge rule; the observed arm must lie outside the null. This rules out degenerate generators; it does not establish an extraction or transfer reading. \emph{Where the decision space includes abstention and the statistic is a proper score, this probe must be restricted to the items on which both compared arms are decisive}---otherwise it measures differential abstention rather than binding, for the reason derived in Section~\ref{sec:battery-refuted}.
\item[E. Sole-discriminator map] Leave-one-coordinate-out over the contract. If some coordinate is never the sole discriminator in any stratum, the ablation describes the generator, not the contract.
\item[F. Construction-vs-measurement decomposition] Attribute the headline gain by stratum. A control arm scoring \emph{exactly} $0.000$ on a stratum is the signature of strata built so the control cannot see the discriminating coordinate: that share of the gain is constructed, not measured.
\item[G. Text destruction] Replace every task surface with noise and recompute everything. If gold and coordinates are unchanged, the benchmark contains no extraction problem and no arm is solving one. For any extraction reading, destroying the text must destroy performance. \emph{Compare the pair (decision, structure signature), never the decision alone}: an arm sitting at a degenerate operating point holds its decision constant under any perturbation, so a decision-only comparison reports scramble-invariance for an extractor that demonstrably reads the text. Measured in the epoch of Section~\ref{sec:multifamily}: changed fraction $1.000$ on the registered pair against $0.005$ on the decision alone.
\item[H. Matched-pair surface attribution] Render the same latent draw through two disjoint surface banks. With structure held fixed, any score difference is attributable to surface wording; this decomposes measured error into structural difficulty versus lexicon coverage.
\end{description}

Probes A--G were discovered by auditing the first two instruments below; the third instrument registered A--G analogues (variance, seed-spread, negative-control gates) \emph{before} outcome access, plus H as an auxiliary control. All battery executions are same-context analysis and are labelled as such.

\subsection{Case 1: the six-family gate passed---and was non-falsifiable}
\label{sec:six-family}

A six-family robustness extension of the objective known-world lane (Section~\ref{sec:known-world}) was frozen as a separate confirmatory coordinate and executed exactly once: seed \texttt{2026081212}, $n=810$ (360 accept / 360 reject / 90 \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK}), with freeze integrity verified by blob hash on the execution host before execution. Every registered gate passed with empty gate reasons: positive family residuals $6/6$; exact two-sided sign test $p=0.03125$; paired binary-Brier gain $0.360$ with item-bootstrap 95\% interval $[0.3267,0.3933]$ against the registered $0.05$ MDE; no retention harm and no false-accept harm in any family, with the full arm retaining every valid transfer at zero invalid false-accepts.

The audit battery then showed that this pass carries no generalization content (\path{research/paper2_six_family_audit_v1/}):

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Probe A failed.} The confirmatory runner binds the full arm to the verifier itself: the full arm \emph{is} the gold function, with binary probabilities fixed at $0.98/0.02$, so its per-item Brier loss is a constant $0.0004$---measured variance $1.24\times10^{-37}$. The registered ``paired'' gain is the mechanism arm's absolute loss minus a constant.
\item \textbf{Probe B failed.} Twelve of twelve arbitrary non-registered seeds reproduce $6/6$ positive families, $p=0.03125$ and a supported verdict. The registered $p$-value is a property of the generator's item strata; a gate no seed can fail is not a test.
\item \textbf{Probe F: two thirds of the gain is construction.} The two strata whose discriminating coordinate is by design invisible to the mechanism-only control score mechanism exact-accuracy \emph{exactly} $0.000$ and together supply $66.7\%$ of the headline advantage; family-level gains are quantized at exactly $0.48$ and $0.24$.
\item \textbf{Probe G: the benchmark contains no extraction problem.} Replacing every source and target text with random noise leaves the gold decision and all six coordinates unchanged on $810/810$ cases (only a lexical diagnostic changes, $762/810$). The coordinates are read from pre-parsed public fields---string equality, integer comparison, a stored boolean, a float threshold. No arm except the lexical diagnostic reads the text at all.
\item \textbf{Probes C--D bound what remains.} A trivial \texttt{always\_reject} arm also attains zero false-accepts, so zero false-accept alone is worthless; the defensible quantity is the joint property (full retention at zero false-accept with correct abstention), which no trivial arm attains. The equal-$n$ shuffle null (200 reps) confirms the generator produces varying task-specific labels---and, given probe G, nothing more.
\end{itemize}

The honest verdict, preserved in the audit artifact, is \textsc{scoped}, not broad generalization: the executed extension demonstrates that the frozen generator, gate and serialization pipeline run to completion and reproduce their registered outputs. It does not support a cross-family transfer law, and given probe~G the finding ``which applicability coordinates a fail-closed gate must enforce'' reduces to arithmetic---checking one of six independent conditions misses violations of the other five.

\subsection{Case 2: a perfect score that measured serialization fidelity}
\label{sec:controlled-witness}

A controlled-witness extraction instrument rendered structural tasks into templated text and measured whether an extractor could recover the applicability coordinates: $n=1296$ base tasks, 2592 text surfaces, gold 576/576/144. The full controlled extractor scored exact decision $1.0$, invalid false-accept $0.0$, abstention recall $1.0$; the strongest non-extraction parent scored $0.889$; a ten-mutation panel (dropped fields, span-hash tampering, unknown-state coercions) was caught in full (\path{research/paper2_controlled_witness_extraction_v1/}).

Probe G refuted the extraction reading. The renderer emits \texttt{Label~::~serialized-value} lines carrying the structural records verbatim, and the extractor parses them back: the render/extract composition is the \emph{identity} on the structural record, recovered byte-identically on $2592/2592$ surfaces, so exact decision $1.0$ is entailed by parse success. Scrambling the prose changes every rendered surface ($1296/1296$) and changes nothing else: gold unchanged $1296/1296$, full arm unchanged $2592/2592$. The conditional promotion terminal was refuted as an extraction claim; what survives is deliberately narrower and is preserved as such---the mutation panel is a valid test of \emph{fail-closed parser behaviour} under field omission and tampering, an honest terminal of serialization-interface fail-closure, not witness extraction from prose.

\subsection{Case 3: a falsifiable instrument that failed honestly---template inversion}
\label{sec:prose-instrument}

The shared mechanism of cases 1 and 2 is that the answer travelled alongside the text in a pre-parsed sibling field, so no arm ever read the text. The successor prose-transfer instrument was therefore registered, \emph{before outcome access}, to the repair specification: an instrument is probative for extraction only if destroying the text destroys performance. Its protocol was frozen as its own commit before any instrument, extractor or runner code existed; gold lives in a latent specification no arm receives and is defined by what the text says---a hedged or elided coordinate is gold-\texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK}, because that is what a perfect reader of that text would conclude. Seven gates were registered: a lower-bound advantage gate (G1), a strict ceiling $<1.00$ (G2), an error-diversity requirement of at least three registered ambiguity classes (G3), a joint retention/false-accept property no trivial arm attains (G4), paired variance in both arms (G5, closing probe A), a twelve-seed spread requirement (G6, closing probe B), and mandatory failure of all three negative controls---scrambled text, shuffled gold, trivial arms (G7). The probe-F repair also held by construction: the violated coordinate is sampled rather than assigned per stratum, and each of the six coordinates appears as sole discriminator (qoi 54, relation 52, effect 52, boundary 44, direction 43, precondition 43 items), so no arm is zeroed by construction.

Executed once on the heldout confirmatory set ($n=576$): the instrument \emph{reads its text}---scrambling changes every surface and collapses full-arm exact accuracy from $0.9722$ to $0.2500$. The collapse landed \emph{below} the registered shuffle-null interval $[0.3385,0.4149]$ rather than inside it, because on noise the extractor fails closed to abstention on every item; this deviation from the registered NC1 wording is recorded as a deviation, not smoothed over---the operative requirement, that G1 fail under scrambled text, held. G1, G2, G4, G5, G6 and G7 passed (advantage $0.4722$, McNemar $p=1.8\times10^{-80}$, bootstrap $[0.4323,0.5156]$; twelve seeds, nine distinct advantage values in $[0.4653,0.4809]$; all three negative controls failed the gate as required). One honest qualification: the registered strongest non-extraction parent resolved to a constant arm (exact $0.500$), so the frozen advantage is measured against a trivial baseline; against the strongest text-reading parent (keyword polarity, exact $0.4601$) the advantage is $0.5122$---both numbers are stated because the gate as frozen picked the trivial one.

\textbf{G3 failed, and that is the result.} All 55 coordinate errors fall in exactly one of seven registered ambiguity classes---the qualitative-degree class whose confirmatory lexicon was deliberately held out from development (53 relation errors, 2 effect errors); the other six classes show zero errors across 3{,}077 realizations. Outside the held-out degree lexicon, the extractor inverts the renderer perfectly. The auxiliary matched-pair control (probe H) sharpens the attribution: rendering the same seeds through the development and heldout surface banks holds the latent draw fixed ($192/192$ identical specifications, $192/192$ identical gold) while changing every rendered text ($192/192$); the extractor scores $1.0000$ on the development surface and $0.9792$ on the heldout surface. The entire measured error is attributable to surface lexicon, not structural difficulty. The frozen terminal is preserved verbatim: \path{INSTRUMENT_NOT_PROBATIVE__TEMPLATE_INVERSION}---what the instrument currently measures is how much of the surface its author templated. The $0.9722$ is therefore not a result about extraction, and G1's large, highly significant advantage does not rescue it; that is why the gate was registered two-sided. The gate discriminated. It just did not pass. (\path{research/paper2_prose_transfer_v1/}.)

The registered revival specification is part of the negative record: a successor's renderer must not be authored by whoever writes the extractor (the single-author coupling produced the zero-error classes); every ambiguity class needs a held-out realization, not just one; and the ceiling must not be raised---a successor reporting a \emph{lower} exact score with errors spread across classes is strictly better evidence. Explicitly rejected as a repair: widening the extractor's cue lists, which would move the score toward $1.00$ and the error count toward zero, i.e.\ further into template inversion while looking like an improvement.

\subsection{Case 4: the shuffle null measured abstention, not binding}
\label{sec:battery-refuted}

The three cases above audit instruments with the battery. This one audits the battery. Probe~D was instantiated for the external-corpus epochs of Section~\ref{sec:arn-negative} as a shuffled-gold negative control: permute the gold column, recompute the registered paired statistic, and declare the instrument non-probative if it still clears the minimum detectable effect. Under the frozen scoring map that probe does not measure what it was registered to measure, and the demonstration is arithmetic rather than empirical.

Write the map as $p(\textsc{accept})=0.98$, $p(\textsc{reject})=0.02$, $p(\texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK})=0.5$, and let the permutation preserve the class balance---which it does exactly here, since every corpus row contributes one gold-\textsc{accept} and one gold-\textsc{reject} pair, so any permutation of the column leaves the balance untouched. Then for an item on which an arm answers \textsc{accept} the gold it is scored against is \textsc{accept} or \textsc{reject} with probability one half each, giving expected Brier $\tfrac12(0.98-1)^2+\tfrac12(0.98-0)^2 = 0.4804$; for an item on which it answers \textsc{reject}, $\tfrac12(0.02-1)^2+\tfrac12(0.02-0)^2 = 0.4804$. The two are \emph{equal}, so a decisive arm's expected loss under the shuffle is independent of both its accept rate and its accuracy. An abstention scores $\tfrac12(0.5-1)^2+\tfrac12(0.5-0)^2 = 0.25$. An arm abstaining at rate $r$ therefore scores $0.4804-0.2304\,r$, and the probe's statistic has expectation
\[
\mathbb{E}\left[\text{shuffled-gold advantage}\right] \;=\; 0.2304\,(r_{\text{witness}}-r_{\text{control}}),
\]
plus noise from the single realized permutation. It is a measurement of \emph{differential abstention}. It is neither sound---an arm that cannot read the gold field at all fails it by abstaining often enough---nor complete, since a genuine leak smaller than the abstention term is masked.

\paragraph{The repair, and the two-sided test that validates it.} Restrict the statistic to the items on which \emph{both} arms are decisive. The abstention term vanishes identically, while a leaking arm keeps leaking on every item it answers, so the leak signal is untouched; the probe narrows its population rather than lowering its threshold, and it is fail-closed, reporting \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK} when no item is jointly decisive rather than passing by absence. Validation asserts the no-alarm case first, because a probe that only ever alarms is not validated by an alarm. An arm whose decision is $\mathrm{sha256}$ of the item identity---gold is never one of its inputs, so its independence is structural rather than estimated---was swept over abstention rates: the original probe fires at rates $0.4$ and $0.6$ ($0.0725$ and $0.1214$ against a $0.05$ threshold) on an instrument with nothing to detect, and the repaired probe clears it at every rate. A planted leak fires both probes from $q=0.3$ upward ($0.1177$), including when masked by abstention at $r=0.6$ ($0.1130$ repaired). Fourteen known-answer tests, all passing.

\paragraph{What this costs the programme, and what it returns.} One previously filed terminal is withdrawn. A successor reducer in the external-corpus line terminated at \path{BATTERY_FAILED__INSTRUMENT_NOT_PROBATIVE} on a shuffled-gold advantage of $0.1347$; its measured abstention rate was $0.5629$, for which the identity predicts $0.1297$ against a single-permutation noise scale near $0.012$. That instrument's battery failure is accounted for by abstention alone and is not evidence of label leakage, so the non-probative reading of it is withdrawn as unsupported. The reading is withdrawn; the receipt is not edited, and the arms it governed are not promoted---re-reading that instrument requires executing the full battery under the repaired probe in a new versioned epoch. Nothing here reverses a confirmatory terminal, and in particular the two \path{NEGATIVE__CAPABILITY_ABSENT} terminals of Section~\ref{sec:arn-negative} did not turn on this probe.

What the case returns is the requirement itself: a negative control defined on a proper scoring rule over a decision space that includes abstention must be evaluated on a population where abstention cannot contribute, or it silently becomes a measurement of how often the arm declined to answer. We had registered probe~D, executed it, and read its verdicts for two epochs before deriving what it computes.

\subsection{Design requirements distilled}
\label{sec:design-requirements}

For any transfer benchmark whose gate is meant to be probative, the four cases above force the following requirements, each traceable to a measured failure: no comparison arm may be the gold function (case~1, probe~A); the registered gate must be failable, demonstrated by exhibiting a failing seed (case~1, probe~B); discriminating coordinates must be sampled, not assigned per stratum (case~1, probe~F; repaired in case~3); the task surface must require recovery, not lookup---destroying it must destroy performance (cases~1--2, probe~G; achieved in case~3); each coordinate must be the sole discriminator in some stratum (case~1, probe~E; achieved in case~3); false-accept must always be reported paired with valid-transfer retention (case~1, probe~C); and error attribution must separate structural difficulty from surface coverage via matched pairs and class-diverse held-out realizations (case~3, probes~G3/H). Case~4 adds two that constrain the probes rather than the instruments: a negative control defined on a proper scoring rule over a decision space including abstention must be restricted to jointly decisive items (probe~D), and a text-destruction probe must compare the pair (decision, structure signature) rather than the decision alone (probe~G). An instrument can satisfy all of the falsifiability requirements and still fail its content gate honestly---case~3---which is exactly what the battery is for; and a probe can satisfy its registration and still measure the wrong quantity---case~4---which is why the battery must be turned on itself and why we report the derivation rather than only the repaired numbers.
