\section{The natural-domain lane: preserved negative history and the open coordinate}
\label{sec:natural-domain}

Everything in this section is either preserved negative history or an explicitly open coordinate. No natural-domain result is claimed.

\subsection{Design vocabulary}

The natural-domain benchmark independently manipulates surface-semantic similarity and structural similarity, giving four cells: Q1 (high/high), Q2 (low semantic, high structural), Q3 (high semantic, low structural) and Q4 (low/low). Q2 and Q3 are load-bearing: a useful structural gate should license Q2 transfers, where vocabulary is deliberately distant but the load-bearing relation/invariant structure is preserved, and reject Q3 decoys, where embedding similarity is high but the structure or boundary that justifies transfer is broken. A deterministic six-case conformance receipt (one Q2 and one Q3 case in each of three mechanism families: queue stability, positive feedback, threshold cascade) shows the implementation expresses the intended distinction---the semantic-only rule is wrong on all six hard cases by construction, the witness gate correct on all six. This is a software-conformance result, not a statistical generalization result, and provides no estimate of performance on naturally occurring mappings.

\subsection{The open coordinate, stated precisely}
\label{sec:open-coordinate}

Before any external judgement, a label-blind power study registered a primary paired Brier-reduction MDE of $0.05$ and simulated paired bootstrap/sign-flip resolution under plausible item-level noise. The frozen 16-item packet is \emph{underpowered} for that MDE: adequate resolution requires roughly $n\approx48$ items across the registered noise envelope. The lane is therefore scoped \path{CONFIRMATORY_PACKET_POWER_LIMITED}: indistinguishable outcomes are inconclusive rather than negative evidence, confirmatory refutation claims remain blocked when intervals include zero, the power limitation is not repaired by the demoted path, and the item set cannot be enlarged post-label and represented as the same confirmatory epoch.

The binding requirement is an externally labelled natural-domain packet at $n\approx48$ whose label authors are independent of the instrument's author. Two routes are admissible, and both are governed by the same admission gate (Section~\ref{sec:open-programme}): a solicited independent-annotation packet under the open successor issue \#359 (two genuinely external annotators, a distinct adjudicator, a distinct provenance auditor); or a published third-party-labelled corpus whose items fit the transfer-instrument shape, where author-independence holds mechanically because the labels were authored before and outside this programme.

\subsection{The corpus route, executed: a measured negative}
\label{sec:arn-negative}

The second route has now been executed once, end to end, under a protocol frozen before first dataset contact (\path{research/paper2_external_corpus_v1/}). A bounded search selected ARN \cite{sourati2024arn}: 1{,}095 query--choice--choice triples over the four quadrants of near/far surface similarity against analogy/distractor status, with gold system mappings deriving from shared proverbs in the crowdworked ePiC corpus and triples vetted by the ARN authors---labels whose authors are mechanically independent of this programme. The registered deterministic reducer was admitted by the implemented admission gate at external-label kind (scramble-sensitive; obstruction-surfacing on the known-answer calibration source), the schema was bound by a committed amendment before any arm scoring, and the falsifiability battery of Section~\ref{sec:battery} passed in full: text destruction changed the witness arm's output on 1{,}542 of 1{,}542 confirmatory pairs and collapsed its accuracy to the shuffled-gold null; the shuffled-gold negative control failed the advantage gate as required; no trivial arm attained the joint property; both compared arms carried per-item loss variance.

Because the battery passed, the outcome is a measurement, and the outcome is negative: \path{NEGATIVE__CAPABILITY_ABSENT}. On 1{,}542 held-out pairs (split by proverb so no system mapping crosses the development/confirmatory boundary), the witness arm's paired-Brier advantage over the strongest frozen non-structural control was $-0.016$ with item-bootstrap 95\% interval $[-0.0498,+0.0162]$---the upper bound below the registered $0.05$ MDE---and its joint property failed with valid-transfer retention $0.023$ at invalid false-accept $0.044$ (reported as a pair, per probe~C). On the Q2-analogue quadrant, far analogies, the gate retained $1.3\%$ ($n=386$). The mechanism is exactly the extraction gap this paper names: cross-domain analogies share almost no surface vocabulary, and an admissible deterministic reducer sees only surface roles, so the fail-closed gate collapses to near-total rejection at chance-level Brier.

This result relocates the open coordinate rather than discharging it. The residual is \emph{not} label availability: third-party labels exist, were acquired, and were used at a sample size ($n=1{,}542 \gg 48$; CI half-width $\pm0.033$) that leaves no power excuse. The residual is a measured extraction-capability gap: no admissible reducer in this programme recovers system-level structure from natural narratives. The named successor was a capable learned extractor, owing the same admission gate plus a contamination declaration. That successor has now been built and read, and the next subsection reports what it measured.

\subsection{The learned successor, executed: the negative bounds a family}
\label{sec:multifamily}

Attributing a negative to the weakness of one reducer is cheap, and this programme had done it four times: each successor in the line repaired the stage its parent's attribution named, and each terminated again. The fifth epoch was therefore designed to answer a different question---whether the residual survives the \emph{class} of extractor the previous subsection names, rather than one more instance of it---by registering a set of correspondence families and reading them in a single confirmatory execution (\path{research/paper2_capability_frontier_v5/}).

Three arms were registered pairwise and absolute, exactly as the parent registered its own: a structural witness taking predicate--argument triples from a dependency parse and scoring them by instance-paired greedy correspondence over sentence-encoder embeddings, so that every score term consumes a specific (query-instance, candidate-instance) pair and none is computable from one narrative alone; a vocabulary-masked witness replacing every content token with its coarse class and syntactic role, which destroys the shared surface vocabulary by construction and so tests directly whether surface is what defeats the structural arms; and, as the strongest calibrated semantic control, whole-narrative encoder cosine---the arm this paper's own stopping rule names, since the programme narrows if the structural score adds no transfer information beyond it. Three further functionals measured during development were registered as diagnostics entering no terminal, so the epoch cannot be read as searching a space and reporting its best cell. Contamination is declared and is symmetric: both trained components may have seen ARN, and the semantic control uses the same encoder as the witnesses, so contamination inflates them together and cannot manufacture the witness-over-control advantage that is the registered statistic.

Admission passed at external-label kind and the battery passed in full under the repaired probe~D of Section~\ref{sec:battery-refuted}. The terminal is \path{NEGATIVE__CAPABILITY_ABSENT} with scope \textsc{multi-family}. The threshold-free discrimination is the honest statement of it:

Every arm sits at or below chance, and every arm is worse on far analogies than on near. Destroying the surface vocabulary does not recover structure: the masked witness reaches chance on the near slice and stays anti-predictive on the far one. Together with the deterministic lineage this bounds lexical, typed-structural, predicate--argument, sentence-encoder and vocabulary-masked correspondence at once, on third-party labels, at a sample size with no power excuse, under a passing battery. The objection that only a weak reducer was tried is discharged.

\paragraph{Why every arm is anti-predictive, and what the successor now owes.} ARN constructs its distractors to be surface-similar to the query while its far analogies share only abstract structure. Every correspondence functional executed in this programme, deterministic or learned, is monotone in some similarity between query and candidate, and a similarity-monotone functional is therefore driven the wrong way exactly where analogical transfer is hardest. The successor now owed is not a better extractor of the same shape: it is a correspondence that is \emph{not} monotone in similarity. We note explicitly that inverting the decision on the far slice would exceed chance on this corpus; it was forbidden in the protocol before outcome access and was not run, because it would exploit the corpus construction rather than measure analogical structure, and we record the exclusion so that its absence is auditable rather than merely absent.

\paragraph{The gate that did the work.} Both witnesses returned a \emph{positive} paired-Brier advantage over the strongest control---$+0.027$ with interval $[0.004,0.050]$ against the registered $0.05$ MDE, and $+0.033$---while both were anti-predictive. The advantage is not the witness being good but the control being worse: the semantic control was selected as strongest on development at $0.557$ exact and falls to $0.468$ on the confirmatory split, its threshold having overfit. Read alone, the advantage statistic would have reported a near-significant win for an arm with AUC $0.43$. What refused it was the joint property: the structural witness sits at the all-reject corner, retaining $0.1\%$ of valid transfers, and the masked witness at the all-accept corner, false-accepting $99\%$ of invalid ones. Neither is a gate; both are constants wearing a threshold, and only the two-sided form distinguishes them from one. This is probe~C---false-accept always reported paired with valid-transfer retention---standing between the programme and a false positive on live data, and it is the strongest evidence for that requirement in this paper.
