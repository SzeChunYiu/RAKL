## The paper asks three questions, not one

### Q1 — structural-state information

Does a checkpoint-bound, typed structural learner state predict the vector of future transfer gain/harm from a candidate exposure **beyond** loss/perplexity, gradient or influence proxies, missing-skill state, semantic diversity, and the strongest fair skill-/model-aware parents?

The target is incremental predictive information under fresh/cross-fitted outcomes, not merely correlation with the training data used to construct the state.

### Q2 — allocation decision value

Conditional on Q1 containing incremental information, can a policy convert it into better fresh transfer per **total** cost than Static Structural and the strongest adaptive parent while satisfying noncompensatory composition, boundary, hostile-near-miss, retention and negative-transfer limits?

The already-frozen five-arm 7B Phase-2 protocol is one preregistered test of Q2. It must not be edited after outcome access or replaced by the newer research challenger.

### Q3 — train-to-inference identity reuse

Does reusing the **exact same registered structural identity** at inference reduce fresh transfer/re-derivation cost relative to a matched system that receives the training benefit without that identity object?

A Q2 policy win does not prove Q3. A Q2 policy loss does not refute Q1.
