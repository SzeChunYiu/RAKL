\section{Current executable evidence, preserved negatives, and repaired successors}\label{app:current-evidence}

The formal results in this paper are accompanied by a ladder of deterministic known-answer experiments. These experiments test whether specific state distinctions matter to the registered decisions; they are not measurements of language-model accuracy. The current ladder contains six positive cells. At L0, context-aligned projection reduces false canonicalization from $0.50$ to $0$ while retaining a true-canonicalization rate of $0.75$. At L1, typed composition reduces unlicensed composition from $0.50$ to $0$ with valid-update acceptance $0.75$. At L2, obstruction-retaining gluing reduces wrong gluing from $0.25$ to $0$ with valid-gluing acceptance $0.833$. At L3, certificate-gated authority reduces authority leakage from $0.50$ to $0$ while valid upgrades remain accepted at $0.75$. At L4, distil-and-navigate solves every registered medium/loose-budget primary world in the frozen panel, compared with $0.323$ for budgeted lexical raw reading; both arms fail on the deliberately tight $60$-world resource floor. At L5, bounded saturation preserves the registered completeness target while saving a mean $0.353$ of the $T_{\max}$ retrieval budget, with strict savings in $79.25\%$ of worlds. Each cell was frozen before execution and remains scoped to its known-world generator and registered comparator.

The projection result reported in the main text uses the repaired v2 benchmark. The development panel has 28 cases across 14 decision families and does not expose an answer-bearing family label to the candidate projection. A ten-coordinate authority basis separates the registered decision twins, and every coordinate is individually necessary on the panel. Simpler compressed projections retain avoidable collisions. A stronger truth-maintenance/provenance/revision diagnostic parent closes part of the gap, so the result is not stated as a generic expressiveness theorem over all rule or logic systems.

The downstream bounded-saturation solve-enablement study on 112 held-out Mathlib tasks remains preserved as its original null: solve rate was $0.357$ for the saturation arm and $0.313$ for the matched-budget uniform arm, with exact McNemar $p=0.227$. A later power audit reproduced that result and found only 11 discordant pairs; at the observed discordant-pair direction the design had approximately $15\%$ power, with roughly 37 discordant pairs required for $80\%$ power. The null is therefore not reclassified as positive evidence; it motivates a separately frozen, power-sized successor rather than a threshold change.

A second historical negative has already produced a successful successor. The frozen source-monitoring repetition attack originally normalized ten submissions to eight identities, giving repetition ratio $0.20$ and failing the unchanged $0.50$ gate. The registered repair changed only source-identity and lineage resolution: scheme-aware canonicalization now maps the same attack corpus to two canonical identities, repetition ratio $0.80$, and passes the unchanged gate. All registered controls pass: eight distinct benign sources remain eight, all six near-miss pairs stay separate, arXiv versions remain distinct entities joined to one lineage root, and equivalent DOI surface forms collapse correctly. Cross-venue identity such as DOI-to-arXiv equivalence remains declaration-bound rather than guessed from strings.

These experiments illustrate the paper's evidence discipline and its recursive repair rule. A positive known-world cell establishes a scoped mechanism behavior under its generator and comparator. A null or negative closes only that realization; it may seed a materially different, pre-registered successor, but its original receipt remains immutable. Neither kind of result can mint scientific authority by itself, and none is silently generalized from a deterministic panel to natural scientific reasoning.
