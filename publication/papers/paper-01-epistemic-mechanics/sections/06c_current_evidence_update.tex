\section{Current executable evidence and preserved negatives}\label{app:current-evidence}

The formal results in this paper are accompanied by a ladder of deterministic known-answer experiments. These experiments test whether specific state distinctions matter to the registered decisions; they are not measurements of language-model accuracy. The current ladder contains six positive cells. At L0, context-aligned projection reduces false canonicalization from $0.50$ to $0$ while retaining a true-canonicalization rate of $0.75$. At L1, typed composition reduces unlicensed composition from $0.50$ to $0$ with valid-update acceptance $0.75$. At L2, obstruction-retaining gluing reduces wrong gluing from $0.25$ to $0$ with valid-gluing acceptance $0.833$. At L3, certificate-gated authority reduces authority leakage from $0.50$ to $0$ while valid upgrades remain accepted at $0.75$. At L4, distil-and-navigate solves every registered medium/loose-budget primary world in the frozen panel, compared with $0.323$ for budgeted lexical raw reading; both arms fail on the deliberately tight $60$-world resource floor. At L5, bounded saturation preserves the registered completeness target while saving a mean $0.353$ of the $T_{\max}$ retrieval budget, with strict savings in $79.25\%$ of worlds. Each cell was frozen before execution and remains scoped to its known-world generator and registered comparator.

The projection result reported in the main text uses the repaired v2 benchmark. The development panel has 28 cases across 14 decision families and does not expose an answer-bearing family label to the candidate projection. A ten-coordinate authority basis separates the registered decision twins, and every coordinate is individually necessary on the panel. Simpler compressed projections retain avoidable collisions. A stronger truth-maintenance/provenance/revision diagnostic parent closes part of the gap, so the result is not stated as a generic expressiveness theorem over all rule or logic systems.

Two negative cells are equally important to the present boundary. First, the non-circular downstream bounded-saturation study on 112 held-out Mathlib tasks remains a null: solve rate was $0.357$ for the saturation arm and $0.313$ for the matched-budget uniform arm, with exact McNemar $p=0.227$. Its frozen revival remains a separate epoch; the original result is not reclassified. Second, the first source-monitoring repetition attack is negative under its registered identity normalizer: ten submitted identifiers yielded eight normalized identities, repetition ratio $0.2$, below the frozen $0.5$ gate. The next mechanism must therefore change source-identity and lineage resolution rather than the threshold.

These experiments illustrate the paper's evidence discipline. A positive known-world cell establishes a scoped mechanism behavior under its generator and comparator. A null or negative closes only that realization. Neither kind of result can mint scientific authority by itself, and none is silently generalized from a deterministic panel to natural scientific reasoning.
