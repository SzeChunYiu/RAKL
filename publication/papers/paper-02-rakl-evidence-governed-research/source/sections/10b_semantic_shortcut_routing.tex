\section{Obstruction--transformation memory and invention-last routing}
\label{sec:semantic-shortcut}

RAKL v3 now exposes a structural experience view whose reusable atom is not a whole paper, topic label or free-form lesson, but a scoped obstruction--transformation episode
\begin{equation}
O\xrightarrow{T}O'.
\end{equation}
Here $O$ records roles, relations, constraints, failure mechanisms, invariants to preserve, a desired transition and forbidden losses; $T$ records a transformation together with its source preconditions; and $O'$ records the resulting relations or state under the episode's source evidence. Episodes are content-bound and retain source authority, provenance, breakpoints and supersession history. They are a retrieval projection over accumulated experience rather than a new scientific-authority store.

\subsection{The shortcut question}

For an active research obstruction, the system asks a narrower question than ``what paper is similar?'':

\begin{quote}
Has this relational obstruction---and a transformation that breaks it---occurred anywhere in the registered knowledge/experience universe?
\end{quote}

The query deliberately normalizes away domain vocabulary before ranking. Lexical or embedding similarity may help nominate sources, but it cannot certify target transport. A source event may be valid while its reuse in the target is invalid.

The strict router is invention-last:
\begin{equation}
\texttt{SEARCH}\rightarrow\texttt{JUMP}\rightarrow\texttt{GLUE}\rightarrow\texttt{LIFT},
\end{equation}
with \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK} whenever memory identity, target mapping, composition, coverage or residual evidence is insufficient.

\paragraph{SEARCH.}
A directly applicable same-domain episode is preferred. Every enabling source precondition must be mapped or repaired; forbidden target losses block reuse; and the recorded transformation must cover the complete desired transition before it can count as a complete route.

\paragraph{JUMP.}
If no direct route survives, cross-domain episodes may be transported only through an explicit structural mapping witness. The witness binds source-to-target roles, shared relations and constraints, complete source-precondition accounting, material disanalogies and target-validation obligations. This makes analogy a proposal compressor rather than an authority shortcut.

\paragraph{GLUE.}
Verified source episodes whose effects are individually partial remain useful. They are not allowed to masquerade as complete SEARCH/JUMP routes. A composition is admissible only when the recorded effects jointly cover the desired transition and a separate composition witness fixes operation order, interfaces, incompatibility checks and target validation.

\paragraph{LIFT.}
New representation/operator invention is permitted only after the registered SEARCH, JUMP and GLUE boundary is explicitly exhausted or blocked. The exhaustion record must account for retrieved candidates, bind cross-problem coverage and show a repeated residual feature across at least two distinct failed attempts. The output is a \emph{MissingTransformationSpecification}: a typed statement of what a downstream invention must preserve, break, expose, reduce and validate. It is not itself the invented tool and grants no scientific or method-promotion authority.

\subsection{Why the route sits between experience and candidate generation}

The obstruction--transformation review occurs after the dual experience-memory query and before candidate proposal. This chronology closes two common loopholes. First, a candidate cannot be invented and then justified retrospectively by finding a vaguely similar source. Second, a local retrieval miss cannot be redescribed as evidence that a new concept is necessary. The relevant memory snapshot and search boundary are frozen before the proposal whose provenance they are meant to explain.

For strict mathematical research the pre-candidate chain is therefore
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
&\text{context/analogy fibre}
\rightarrow \text{success+failure experience review}\\
&\rightarrow \text{obstruction--transformation review}
\rightarrow \text{hash-chained decision trace}\\
&\rightarrow \text{candidate proposal/falsification}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Truth, novelty and research value remain downstream, separately checked coordinates.

\subsection{Connection to method evolution}

A validated target transformation can later feed two distinct long-horizon stores. Its structural source/target lineage may become a new obstruction--transformation episode; if the method step is genuinely reusable, it may separately be distilled into a scoped \texttt{ResearchTool}. Neither promotion is automatic from one successful target. Conversely, repeated failures can enrich the failure lattice and the residual structure used by a future LIFT decision without becoming a global blacklist.

This closes an experience loop that the earlier v3 architecture left implicit:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\text{experience}
&\rightarrow \text{structural episode memory}
\rightarrow \text{witnessed reuse/composition}\\
&\rightarrow \text{residual-guided specification when reuse is exhausted}\\
&\rightarrow \text{validated candidate}
\rightarrow \text{scoped consolidation}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

The implementation of this loop is not evidence that it improves research performance. A matched efficacy, safety and cost evaluation is separately registered in issue \#401, while issue \#402 owns construction and coverage metrology for a provenance-bound episode corpus.
