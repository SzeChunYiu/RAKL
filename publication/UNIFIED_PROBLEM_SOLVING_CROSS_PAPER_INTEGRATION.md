# Orion Unified Problem-Solving Framework — Cross-Paper Integration Map

Status: proposal-only integration note for publication series V2. This file does not change a paper's empirical status by itself.

## Why this is one framework rather than a seventh parallel theory

The unified problem-solving work is an implementation and research projection across the existing series. Its new sidecars are owned by canonical Orion method surfaces and grant no scientific or theorem authority. The papers should therefore divide responsibility rather than repeating the full architecture.

| Paper | Existing responsibility | Unified problem-solving integration | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I — Epistemic Mechanics** | Evidence, claims, scope, authority, provenance, contradiction, saturation | Supplies the noninterference firewall: map scores, geometry, solver selection, flow and MDD/VSC decisions remain routing/proposal state until a separately verified evidence transition occurs. | No solver-sidecar result changes scientific authority directly. |
| **II — Structural Mechanics** | Directional structural witnesses, preserved/non-preserved properties, QoI/boundaries, GLUE/JUMP | Owns chart/portal preservation, cross-representation transfer contracts and local-to-global interface obligations used by VSC and fibred Orion. | Representation proximity or a portal proposal is not equivalence without a witness/validation contract. |
| **III — Method-Evolution Mechanics** | Episode/failure → diagnosis → lesson/tool → fresh assurance; Self-Orion | Owns consolidation of MDD diagnoses, useful compilation/geometry/verification policies and reusable solver artifacts into method candidates. | Development stress tests cannot self-promote a method; fresh protected transfer remains required. |
| **IV — Structural Learning Mechanics** | Learner-conditioned structural mastery/allocation protocol | May later use transformation-effect/solver-artifact identities as training coordinates only after the Phase-1 generator/instrument is valid. The executed v1 Phase-1 packet is invalidated by a generator defect and is preserved only as negative instrument history. | No adaptive-allocation or structural-learning mechanism conclusion follows from v1. |
| **V — Verified Discovery in Mathematics** | Specification, theorem truth, proof DAG/checkpoints, novelty, research value, verifier trust | Owns the mathematics-specific Verified Transformation Geometry (VTG) hypothesis, exact-transition/partial-map distinction, path-vs-certificate separation, path quotienting and the Phase-0/1 held-out Lean geometry preregistration. | The existence/usefulness of locally navigable mathematical reachability geometry remains an empirical question. |
| **VI — Orion Scientific Research Engine** | Capstone integration of knowledge, solution, experience and self-evolution loops | Owns the systems synthesis: MDD → VSC → map/atlas/geometry/navigation → certificate → authority; fibred multiscale Orion; path-cost algebra; fieldability/reuse/staleness; verification scheduling; reproducible known-world figures. | Current results are architectural invariants and development known-world mechanism tests, not frontier scientific-agent superiority. |

## Figure ownership

The reproducible script `paper/generate_unified_solver_figures.py` produces:

- `unified_solver_architecture`: cross-domain systems figure, primarily Paper VI;
- `unified_field_amortization`: build/reuse economics, Paper VI;
- `unified_local_vs_closed_loop`: local progress versus closed-loop navigation, Papers V/VI;
- `unified_verification_pareto`: multiscale verification tradeoff, Paper VI;
- `unified_path_quotient`: concurrency/path redundancy, Papers V/VI.

All quantitative figures are regenerated from `research/unified_problem_solving_v1/run_known_world_stress.py`. Their captions must state `development known world` or the exact synthetic assumption set; none is a learned-model performance plot.

## Publication-status rule

The unified framework can be described as **implemented and CI-tested** only at an exact commit where the dedicated hardening workflow and ordinary repository tests pass. VTG, MDD, VSC, learned geometry, natural dynamics, multiscale utility and verification scheduling remain empirical-open coordinates until their separately frozen fresh benchmarks execute.
